## What Makes a Soda "Dirty" at Applebee's

A dirty soda is a fountain soda with cream and flavor added. At Applebee's the presets get a flavor shot, a swirl of half-and-half, whipped cream and a cherry. The trend started in Utah soda shops, and Applebee's put its version on the national menu on October 6, 2025.

The $1 option is simpler. You pick any fountain drink and they top it with whipped cream and a cherry. That's the version to get if you like Starry or Diet Pepsi and don't want Mountain Dew.

## The Three Flavors

### Passion Blue Dew

![Blue Mountain Dew dirty soda with whipped cream and a cherry](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-passion-blue-dew-soda-drink.webp)

Mountain Dew Dark Berry Bash, which already tastes of blue raspberry and blackberry, with passion fruit added. It's the sweetest-tasting of the three and, oddly, the lightest.

- **$3.99** · 150 calories

### Mango Dream Dew

![Mango Mountain Dew dirty soda topped with whipped cream](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-mango-dream-dew-drink.webp)

Regular Mountain Dew with a tangy mango flavor. The citrus in the Dew and the mango sit together well, and the cream softens both. Order this one first. PureWow's reviewer ranked it the best of the three.

- **$3.99** · 160 calories

### Cherry Charmed Pepsi

![Pepsi with grenadine, cream swirl, whipped cream and a cherry](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-cherry-charmed-pepsi-drink.webp)

Pepsi and grenadine, which ends up tasting like a creamy cherry cola. It's the only one of the three without Mountain Dew, so it's the pick for anyone who doesn't like Dew.

- **$3.99** · 170 calories

## Graveyard Dirty Soda (Halloween 2026)

Part of this year's Spooky Sips, through November 15, 2026: Mountain Dew with blue raspberry, a creamy swirl, whipped cream, grenadine and crushed OREO cookies. It's the heaviest-looking drink on the soda menu and the only one with a crunch. See the rest of the Halloween drinks on [signature cocktails](/menu/signature-cocktails).

## Caffeine

Two of the three are Mountain Dew, which has more caffeine per ounce than Pepsi. If a child is ordering, or it's late, the Cherry Charmed Pepsi has less. A $1 dirty Starry has none.

## Refills

The cream, whipped cream and cherry go on one glass. Don't expect a refill to come back dirty for free. Ask your server how your restaurant charges before you order a second.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Applebee's Dirty Fountain Sodas?
Fountain sodas with a flavor shot, a half-and-half swirl, whipped cream and a cherry. The three presets are Passion Blue Dew, Mango Dream Dew and Cherry Charmed Pepsi.

### How much is a dirty soda at Applebee's?
About $3.99 for a preset flavor, though some restaurants charge $3.79. Making any fountain soda dirty costs $1 more.

### How many calories are in an Applebee's dirty soda?
150 for Passion Blue Dew, 160 for Mango Dream Dew and 170 for Cherry Charmed Pepsi.

### When did Applebee's start selling dirty sodas?
October 6, 2025.

### What is the Graveyard Dirty Soda?
A Halloween 2026 dirty soda: Mountain Dew with blue raspberry, cream, whipped cream, grenadine and OREO crumbles, through November 15, 2026.

### Can you get a dirty Diet Pepsi at Applebee's?
Yes. Any fountain drink can be made dirty for $1.
