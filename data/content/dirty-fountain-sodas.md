### What is a "Dirty Soda"?

To understand the appeal of Applebee's Dirty Fountain Sodas, we first have to look at where the concept originated. The "dirty soda" trend exploded in the Mountain West region of the United States, particularly in Utah. It started as a way to customize and elevate standard fountain drinks.

The basic premise is simple but genius: take a classic carbonated soda (like cola, lemon-lime, or a fruit-flavored soda), "dirty" it up by adding flavored syrups (such as vanilla, coconut, or fruit purees), and finish it with a splash of cream or a creamy topping. The result is a drink that is fizzy, sweet, rich, and incredibly refreshing. It sits somewhere between a traditional soda and a float or a milkshake.

Applebee's took this viral concept and brought it to the masses, utilizing their exclusive Mountain Dew® flavors as the base to create something entirely unique and deeply craveable.

### The Appeal of the Creamy Topping

The defining characteristic of any dirty soda is the dairy element. Applebee's uses a specially formulated creamy swirl that sits on top of the ice. 

Many people wonder if mixing dairy and carbonated soda results in a strange texture. The answer is a resounding no. When the heavy cream hits the ice-cold, highly carbonated soda, it doesn't curdle. Instead, it slowly cascades down through the ice, creating beautiful ribbons of white throughout the brightly colored drink. 

It mellows out the sharp bite of the carbonation, resulting in a drink that goes down incredibly smooth. It is this specific textural change that has made the dirty soda trend so viralit transforms a standard fountain drink into a premium, crafted beverage experience.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Can I get a Dirty Soda made with Diet Pepsi or Zero Sugar Mountain Dew?**
Currently, the signature Dirty Fountain Sodas are specifically crafted using the Mountain Dew Dark Berry Bash® as the base. However, depending on the bartender or server, you may be able to request the creamy topping and syrups added to a diet beverage for a custom creation.

**Does the cream in a dirty soda curdle?**
No! As long as the soda is ice cold, the creamy topping simply mixes in, creating a smooth, velvety texture without curdling. 

**Are there free refills on Dirty Fountain Sodas?**
Unlike standard fountain sodas, Dirty Fountain Sodas are considered specialty, crafted beverages (similar to a milkshake or a mocktail). Therefore, they typically do not come with free refills. 

**Can I get these drinks to-go?**
Absolutely! Applebee's offers their Dirty Fountain Sodas for takeout and delivery, packaged securely so you can enjoy the viral trend from the comfort of your own home.
