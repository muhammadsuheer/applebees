## You Can Dirty Up Any Soda at Applebee's

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    Applebee's lets you add a creamy swirl, whipped cream and a cherry to any fountain soda for around $1 extra. The three signature Dirty Fountain Sodas  Passion Blue Dew, Cherry Charmed Pepsi and Mango Dream Dew  are preset combinations, but the same treatment can be applied to whichever soda you prefer.
  </p>
</div>

This is the part most menu listings leave out, and it is the difference between a drinks category and a build-your-own option:

- **What gets added:** A swirl of half-and-half, whipped cream on top, and a maraschino cherry. Flavoured syrup goes in on the preset combinations.
- **What it costs:** Roughly a dollar over the price of the soda. Pricing is set by each location, so it varies.
- **Why order a preset instead:** The three on the menu are combinations Applebee's has already balanced  the syrup, the base and the cream are matched. Building your own gives you control; ordering a preset gives you something that has been tested.

## What Is a Dirty Soda?

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    A dirty soda is a fountain drink customised with flavoured syrup and a creamy element  usually cream, half-and-half or coconut milk  often finished with whipped cream. The style began in Utah and has become one of the fastest-growing categories on American restaurant menus.
  </p>
</div>

- **Where it came from:** Utah drink chain Swig coined the term in 2010, building a business on customised fountain sodas. For a decade it stayed regional. TikTok changed that, and so did the reality series *The Secret Lives of Mormon Wives*, which put dirty soda in front of a national audience.
- **Why it works, mechanically:** Cream poured over ice-cold carbonated soda does not curdle. It cascades through the ice in ribbons and softens the sharp edge of the carbonation. The drink ends up somewhere between a soda and a float  fizzy but smooth.
- **How big it has become:** Dirty soda is growing at roughly 42% a year on US restaurant menus, according to [MenuData](https://www.menudata.io/). The share of American eateries serving a soda with cream or milk has risen from 1.5% a decade ago to 2.7%, per [Datassential](https://datassential.com/). Swig's systemwide sales grew nearly 50% in 2024, and the chain now runs more than 140 locations across 16 states.
- **Who else is doing it:** Taco Bell has run dirty versions of Mountain Dew Baja Blast. McDonald's is testing flavoured sodas at over 500 locations according to [Nation's Restaurant News](https://www.nrn.com/). Sonic, Jack in the Box and TGI Fridays have all launched versions. PepsiCo and Coca-Cola have released ready-to-drink dirty sodas for grocery.
- **Applebee's launch:** Applebee's launched its version in 2025 alongside the return of the All You Can Eat menu.

### Passion Blue Dew

![Blue soda with cream swirl, whipped cream and a cherry](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-passion-blue-dew-soda-drink.webp)

Built on Mountain Dew Dark Berry Bash  a blue raspberry and blackberry Mountain Dew  with passion fruit added on top.

It is the sharpest of the three. Passion fruit is genuinely tart, and it sits against a base that is already sweet-tart rather than plain. The cream does more work here than in the other two, because without it the drink reads as sour rather than smooth.

It is also the one people order for the colour. Deep blue with a white swirl running through it is the reason this drink ends up on social media.

- **Profile:** Mountain Dew Dark Berry Bash + Passion Fruit + Cream Swirl + Whipped Cream + Cherry · 150 Cals

### Cherry Charmed Pepsi

![Cherry cola with grenadine, cream swirl and whipped topping](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-cherry-charmed-pepsi-drink.webp)

Pepsi with grenadine, cream and a cherry  the closest of the three to a traditional soda fountain drink.

A small detail worth knowing: grenadine is traditionally made from pomegranate rather than cherry. It carries the colour and the sweet-tart profile people associate with cherry cola, which is why it works here, but it is not cherry syrup.

With the cream mixed through, this is essentially a cola float in a glass. If you like cherry cola, it is the obvious starting point.

- **Profile:** Classic Pepsi + Grenadine + Cream Swirl + Whipped Cream + Cherry · 170 Cals

### Mango Dream Dew

![Mango soda with cream swirl and whipped cream](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-mango-dream-dew-drink.webp)

Mountain Dew's citrus base with a tangy mango twist.

The most straightforward of the three and the easiest to drink. Mango and citrus is a natural pairing, and the cream rounds it into something closer to a mango lassi than a soda. Reviewers tend to rate this one highest of the three for flavour, even though the Passion Blue gets more attention for looks.

- **Profile:** Mountain Dew + Mango Syrup + Cream Swirl + Whipped Cream + Cherry · 160 Cals

## How to Build Your Own Dirty Soda at Applebee's

Since any soda can be dirtied, this is the section that makes the page useful rather than descriptive:

- **Start with the base:** Pepsi and the Mountain Dew range are the fountain options the presets are built on. A cola base gives you a float; a citrus base gives you something brighter.
- **The rule that works:** Match sharp with sweet, or sweet with cream. Grenadine over Pepsi works because tart cuts sweet. Cream over any of them works because fat rounds acid. What does not work is stacking sweet on sweet  a sugary syrup over an already sweet base without enough acid comes out flat.
- **Ask what syrups are available:** Availability varies by location, and there are usually more options behind the bar than the three presets suggest.
- **Requests worth making:** Less ice for more drink, extra cream if you want it closer to a float, or the syrup on the light side if you find the presets too sweet.
- **If you are keeping it lighter:** The cream and whipped topping are where most of the added calories sit. A diet base with a small amount of syrup and no whipped cream is a meaningfully different drink from a full-sugar preset. Whether a location will build that is worth asking rather than assuming.

Published figures for the presets fall in the 150 to 170 calorie range for a standard serving, though calorie counts vary by size and by how much syrup and cream go in. Larger sizes run considerably higher.

Where the calories are: the syrup and the cream. The soda base is what it always was; the additions are what change the number.

## Food Pairings

These are sweet and creamy, so they work against heat and salt rather than alongside sweetness:

- **With spiced food:** Cream is a cooling agent. Buffalo-sauced [Boneless Wings](/menu/appetizers) and the Cajun seasoning on [Fiesta Lime Chicken](/menu/chicken) are where it helps most. The Passion Blue Dew's tartness works particularly well against Buffalo sauce.
- **With something rich and salty:** Carbonation cuts fat, which is why soda and burgers have always worked. Any of the [handcrafted burgers](/menu/handcrafted-burgers) pair straightforwardly, and the Cherry Charmed Pepsi is the closest to a classic burger-and-cola combination.
- **With shareable fried food:** Mango Dream Dew against [Chicken Wonton Tacos](/menu/appetizers)  the tropical notes pick up the sweet Asian chile in the tacos.
- **Not with dessert:** A dirty soda plus a Triple Chocolate Meltdown is two desserts. If you are having both, the unsweetened iced tea is the better drink alongside.

## When Dirty Sodas Are Most Popular

Worth knowing if you are planning a visit, and worth knowing if you run a content calendar:

- **Demand peaks in summer:** Industry visit data for dirty soda chains shows a clear seasonal pattern, with volumes peaking each year in the warmer months as people look for cold sweet drinks. The 2025 summer peak was the largest recorded.
- **Off-season demand is growing too:** Which is what separates a trend from a fad  people are ordering these year-round rather than only in July.
- **Practical implication for the menu:** Limited-time dirty soda flavours tend to appear in spring and summer across the industry. If Applebee's adds seasonal variants, that is when to expect them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is a Dirty Fountain Soda at Applebee's?
A fountain soda with a swirl of half-and-half, whipped cream and a cherry added, for about $1 more. Applebee's offers three preset flavours  Passion Blue Dew, Cherry Charmed Pepsi and Mango Dream Dew  and the same treatment can be applied to any fountain soda.

### Can I dirty up any soda at Applebee's?
Yes. The three signature combinations are presets, but the cream, whipped topping and cherry can be added to whichever fountain soda you choose for around a dollar extra.

### How much do Applebee's Dirty Fountain Sodas cost?
Roughly $1 above the price of the soda itself. Exact pricing is set by each location, since Applebee's restaurants are independently franchised.

### What are the Applebee's dirty soda flavors?
Passion Blue Dew  Mountain Dew Dark Berry Bash with passion fruit. Cherry Charmed Pepsi  Pepsi with grenadine. Mango Dream Dew  Mountain Dew with mango. All three come with a creamy swirl, whipped cream and a cherry.

### How many calories are in an Applebee's dirty soda?
Published figures for a standard serving fall in the 150 to 170 calorie range, with larger sizes higher. Most of the added calories come from the syrup and the cream rather than the soda.

### What is a dirty soda?
A fountain drink customised with flavoured syrup and a creamy element, often finished with whipped cream. The style started with Utah chain Swig in 2010 and is now one of the fastest-growing categories on American restaurant menus, expanding at roughly 42% a year.

### Does the cream curdle in a dirty soda?
No. Over ice-cold carbonated soda the cream cascades through the ice in ribbons rather than curdling. That texture is the point of the format.

### What is in the creamy swirl?
Half-and-half, plus whipped cream on top and a maraschino cherry.

### Can I get a dirty soda with diet soda?
The presets are built on specific bases, but since the treatment can be applied to any fountain soda, a diet base is worth asking about. Availability of individual syrups varies by location.

### Do Applebee's dirty sodas come with free refills?
These are prepared drinks rather than standard self-serve fountain sodas, and refill policy is set by each location. Worth checking with your restaurant.

### Can I order a dirty soda for takeout?
Availability for [takeout](/takeout) and [delivery](/delivery) varies by location. A cream-topped drink also travels less well than a plain soda  the layering that defines it does not survive a sealed cup.

### Is Mountain Dew Dark Berry Bash only at Applebee's?
Dark Berry Bash is a blue raspberry and blackberry Mountain Dew flavour used as the base for the Passion Blue Dew. Availability of specific Mountain Dew flavours varies by outlet.
