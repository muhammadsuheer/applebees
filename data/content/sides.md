## Every Applebee's Side

### Steamed Broccoli

![Steamed broccoli florets served as a vegetable side](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-steamed-broccoli-side.webp)

The lightest side and the least sodium in the section. One thing the numbers give away: 8g of fat on a side of broccoli means it isn't plain steamed. It lists none of the major allergens in our data, but if you're eating strictly plant-based, ask how it's cooked.

- **$3.49** · 100 calories · 200mg sodium · 7g carbs

### Garlicky Green Beans

![Tender whole green beans with garlic](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-garlicky-green-beans-side.webp)

The other vegetable side, 50 calories heavier than broccoli, and tied with fries for the most fiber at 4g. The 12g of fat comes from how they're cooked. No major allergens in our data.

- **$3.49** · 150 calories · 550mg sodium · 4g fiber

### Coleslaw

![Creamy shredded cabbage coleslaw](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-coleslaw-side.webp)

Creamy cabbage slaw. It's the side that cuts through fried food, the way a squeeze of lemon does.

- **$3.49** · 230 calories

### Garlic Mashed Potatoes

![Creamy garlic mashed potatoes served as a side](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-garlic-mashed-potatoes-side.webp)

The middle of the range at 260 calories, and the best side for anything with a sauce worth mopping up. They also travel better than any other side, which makes them the smart swap for fries on a takeout order.

- **$3.49** · 260 calories

### Classic Fries

![Golden seasoned fries served as a side portion](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-classic-french-fries-side.webp)

The default side on burgers and sandwiches. 400 calories, 800mg of sodium and 50g of carbohydrate, with 0g of sugar. Soy is the only allergen they list.

No wheat in the recipe doesn't make them safe for celiac disease. With a serious gluten issue, treat anything from a fryer as exposed to wheat unless the kitchen tells you otherwise.

- **$3.99** · 400 calories · 800mg sodium · contains soy

### Loaded Baked Potato

![Baked potato topped with cheese, bacon, sour cream and chives](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-loaded-baked-potato-side.webp)

A baked potato with cheese, sour cream, bacon and chives. The heaviest side at 450. Put it next to something lean, like the [6 oz. Top Sirloin](/menu/steaks-and-ribs), not a burger.

- **$4.49** · 450 calories

## Sides To Go

Fries are the worst side for takeout. They go soft in a closed box within the drive home. Mashed potatoes and steamed vegetables arrive the way they left. If you're ordering [Carside To Go](/takeout), swap the fries.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What sides does Applebee's have?
Steamed Broccoli, Garlicky Green Beans, Coleslaw, Garlic Mashed Potatoes, Classic Fries and a Loaded Baked Potato. Crispy Pickle Fries are available as part of the Ultimate Trio.

### How much are sides at Applebee's?
$3.49 for most sides, $3.99 for Classic Fries and $4.49 for the Loaded Baked Potato.

### What's the lowest calorie side at Applebee's?
Steamed Broccoli at 100 calories, then Garlicky Green Beans at 150.

### How many calories are in Applebee's fries?
400, with 800mg of sodium and 50g of carbohydrate.

### Can I swap fries for vegetables at Applebee's?
Yes, ask when you order. Swapping Classic Fries for Steamed Broccoli saves 300 calories and 600mg of sodium.

### Are Applebee's fries gluten-free?
There's no wheat in the recipe, but they're fried, so don't count on them for celiac disease.

### What are Applebee's Crispy Pickle Fries?
Fried pickle fries tossed in TAJÍN and chile lime seasoning with Mexi-ranch for dipping. They're 590 calories and one of the picks on the Ultimate Trio.
