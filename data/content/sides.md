## The Supporting Cast: Elevating the Meal with Sauces & Sides

When evaluating a restaurant, the main entrées get all the glory. The steaks, the pastas, and the burgers take up the most real estate on the menu and the plate. However, the true mark of a great kitchen lies in its supporting cast: the **Sides**. 

At Applebee's, the side dishes are not just an afterthought used to fill empty space on the plate. They are carefully crafted components designed to complement, contrast, and elevate the main courses. From the comforting warmth of garlic mashed potatoes to the simple, crisp nutritional balance of steamed broccoli, choosing the right side is crucial to building the perfect meal. Let's break down the most popular options available.

---

### The Ultimate Comfort: Garlic Mashed Potatoes

![Applebee's Garlic Mashed Potatoes](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-garlic-mashed-potatoes-side.webp)

If you are ordering a rich, heavy entrée like the Bourbon Street Steak or the Double Barrel Bone-In Pork Chop, you need a side dish that can hold its own against those massive, savory flavors. Enter the **Garlic Mashed Potatoes**.

#### The Execution
These are not thin, watery potatoes out of a box. Applebee's mashes their potatoes to a creamy, slightly rustic consistency, leaving just enough texture to prove they are real. The potatoes are heavily blended with butter, cream, and roasted garlic. The roasting process mellows out the sharp bite of the garlic, leaving a deeply savory, slightly sweet flavor profile that permeates every single bite. 

At 260 calories and priced at $3.49 (if ordered a la carte), they are the ultimate comfort food side, perfect for soaking up any leftover juices or sauces on your plate.

---

### The Decadent Upgrade: Loaded Baked Potato

![Applebee's Loaded Baked Potato](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-loaded-baked-potato-side.webp)

Sometimes, a side dish is so rich and massive that it borders on becoming an entrée itself. The **Loaded Baked Potato** is the premier upgrade option on the Applebee's menu.

#### The Build
It starts with a massive, fluffy Idaho baked potato, cooked until the skin is slightly crispy and the interior is piping hot and soft. The potato is split open and aggressively "loaded." 
The standard build includes:
- A heavy pat of melting butter.
- A generous handful of shredded cheddar cheese that melts immediately into the hot potato.
- A dollop of cool, tangy sour cream.
- Crispy applewood-smoked bacon bits for a salty crunch.
- Fresh chives for a slight oniony bite and a pop of color.

At 450 calories and $4.49, this side is incredibly filling. It is the absolute best companion to a lean 6 oz. Top Sirloin or a simple order of Grilled Chicken.

---

### The Healthy Balance: Steamed Broccoli

![Applebee's Steamed Broccoli](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-steamed-broccoli.webp)

In an era where diners are increasingly conscious of their nutritional intake, a casual dining menu must offer a clean, healthy counterbalance to the heavy burgers and fried appetizers. The **Steamed Broccoli** serves this exact purpose flawlessly.

#### The Execution
Cooking broccoli in a restaurant setting is a delicate balancing act. If it is undercooked, it is tough and fibrous; if it is overcooked, it becomes mushy and loses its vibrant color. Applebee's steams their broccoli florets to a perfect tender-crisp consistency. It retains a bright, vibrant green color and a satisfying snap when you bite into it. 

It is lightly seasoned to enhance the natural flavor without overwhelming it with salt or butter. At just 100 calories and $3.49, it is the perfect side to pair with a rich dish like the Three-Cheese Chicken Penne (to cut through the heavy cream sauce) or alongside a blackened salmon filet for a perfectly balanced, high-protein meal.

---

### Why Sides Matter

Customizing your sides is the easiest way to manipulate the nutritional profile and the overall "feel" of your dining experience at Applebee's. 
- Swapping fries for broccoli can save you hundreds of calories.
- Upgrading to a loaded baked potato can turn a light lunch into a massive dinner. 
Never overlook the supporting cast; they are the secret to building your perfect Applebee's plate.
