## Applebee's Garlic Mashed Potatoes Near You

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    Applebee's serves garlic mashed potatoes at every location as a standard side, priced around $3.49 on its own and included with many entrées at no extra charge. At 260 calories they are one of the lighter starch sides on the menu.
  </p>
</div>

Availability is universal. Unlike seafood or seasonal items, mashed potatoes are core menu — every Applebee's carries them, and they are the default side on several entrées including the steaks and the blackened salmon.

Three ways to order them:

- **As the included side** with an entrée that comes with a starch — no upcharge, and you can request them in place of fries.
- **A la carte** at around $3.49 if you want an extra portion for the table.
- **As part of a larger order** — they travel well, which is not true of most sides here.

### Reading the Table

- **The full spread is 350 calories:** From 100 for broccoli to 450 for a loaded baked potato. On a menu where most entrées already clear 1,000 calories, the side is where you still have room to decide.
- **Sides are the lowest-sodium part of the menu alongside desserts:** Steamed broccoli carries 200mg and green beans 550mg. For comparison, one appetizer on this menu carries 4,720mg.
- **Fries carry zero sugar and 4g of fiber**, which surprises people. Their cost is calories and sodium, not sugar.

## Detailed Side Item Breakdowns

### Garlic Mashed Potatoes

![Creamy garlic mashed potatoes served as a side](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-garlic-mashed-potatoes-side.webp)

The default side on several Applebee's entrées, including both sirloin cuts and the blackened salmon. Creamy rather than whipped smooth, blended with butter, cream and roasted garlic — roasting is what takes the sharp edge off garlic and leaves it savoury rather than pungent.

At 260 calories they sit in the middle of the range, and they are the best side on the menu for an entrée with sauce or juices worth picking up.

They also travel better than any other side here, which makes them the sensible swap on a [takeout](/takeout) order where fries would arrive soft.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $3.49 · 260 calories

### Classic Fries

![Golden seasoned fries served as a side portion](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-classic-french-fries-side.webp)

The standard side with burgers, sandwiches, tenders and ribs. Cut medium-thick and fried crisp.

At 400 calories they are the heaviest of the common sides, with 800mg of sodium and 50g of carbohydrate. No sugar at all.

The one caveat worth knowing: fries are cooked in shared fryer oil alongside breaded chicken and fish. They contain no wheat themselves, but cross-contact means they are not a safe choice for coeliac disease.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $3.99 · 400 calories

### Steamed Broccoli

![Steamed broccoli florets served as a vegetable side](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-steamed-broccoli-side.webp)

The lightest side on the menu at 100 calories, and the swap that does the most work anywhere on the Applebee's menu.

One thing to know: the published figure includes 8g of fat, which for plain broccoli indicates butter or oil in preparation. Asking for it without butter is a straightforward request and matters if you are eating dairy-free or plant-based.

At 200mg of sodium it is the lowest-sodium item in the category.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $3.49 · 100 calories · gluten-free

### Garlicky Green Beans

![Tender whole green beans sautéed with savory garlic butter](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-garlicky-green-beans-side.webp)

The other vegetable option, and the highest-fiber side at 4g. At 150 calories it sits just above broccoli.

Same note applies: 12g of fat on a plate of green beans indicates butter or garlic oil. Order without if that matters to you.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $3.49 · 150 calories · gluten-free

### Loaded Baked Potato

![Baked potato topped with cheese, bacon, sour cream and chives](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-loaded-baked-potato-side.webp)

The upgrade option, and the heaviest side at 450 calories. A baked potato split and topped with cheese, sour cream, bacon and chives.

Best paired with something lean — a [6 oz. Top Sirloin](/menu/steaks-and-ribs) or grilled chicken — where the plate has room for it. Alongside a burger it turns a large meal into a very large one.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $4.49 · 450 calories

### Coleslaw

![Creamy shredded cabbage coleslaw](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-coleslaw-side.webp)

Served alongside fried items — ribs, tenders and fried seafood — where its acidity does the same job lemon does with rich food.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $3.49 · 230 calories

## Swapping Sides: The Biggest Calorie Change on the Menu

This is the most useful section on the page, and it applies to every entrée Applebee's serves.

Most entrées come with a side included, and swapping it is generally free. That makes it the one change available on every order.

Why this matters more than it sounds. A 300-calorie change is larger than most people achieve by choosing a "lighter" entrée. Ordering the Classic Bacon Cheeseburger with broccoli instead of fries brings it from 1,220 to around 920 — below the Impossible Cheeseburger with fries.

The side does more than the entrée choice does, on almost every order.

### Where the Swap Matters Most

- **On fried entrées:** Tenders, fried seafood and ribs all arrive with fries by default. Those are the plates where the total is already high and the swap has the most room to work.
- **On steaks:** Both sirloins come with mashed potatoes and broccoli as standard, so they are already well set up. Swapping the potato for a second vegetable is the further step.
- **On takeout orders:** Here the swap is about quality as much as calories. Fries are the worst travelling item on the menu — mashed potatoes and steamed vegetables arrive in the condition they left. See [takeout](/takeout) and [delivery](/delivery).

## Dietary Notes

- **Lowest calorie:** Steamed Broccoli at 100.
- **Most fiber:** Garlicky Green Beans at 4g, tied with Classic Fries.
- **Lowest sodium:** Steamed Broccoli at 200mg — the lowest of any item in this category.
- **Gluten:** Steamed Broccoli and Garlicky Green Beans are flagged gluten-free. Fries contain no wheat but share fryer oil with breaded items, so they carry cross-contact risk for coeliac disease.
- **Dairy and plant-based:** The vegetable sides carry fat figures indicating butter or oil in preparation. Ordering them without butter is the fix, and it is a standard request. The mashed potatoes contain dairy and cannot be made dairy-free.
- **Building a plant-based plate:** A plain baked potato without butter or sour cream, steamed vegetables without butter, and a House Salad from the [salads menu](/menu/salads) is how a vegan meal is assembled at Applebee's — there is no vegan entrée.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What sides does Applebee's have?
Applebee's sides include garlic mashed potatoes, classic fries, steamed broccoli, garlicky green beans, coleslaw and a loaded baked potato, plus pickle fries, waffle fries and onion rings at participating locations. Prices run $3.49 to $4.49.

### How much are Applebee's sides?
Between $3.49 and $4.49 a la carte. Most entrées include a side at no extra charge, and swapping it for a different option is generally free.

### Can I swap fries for a vegetable at Applebee's?
Yes, and it is usually free. Swapping fries for steamed broccoli removes around 300 calories — the largest repeatable calorie reduction available on the Applebee's menu.

### What is the lowest calorie side at Applebee's?
Steamed broccoli at 100 calories, followed by garlicky green beans at 150. Broccoli also carries the lowest sodium in the category at 200mg.

### How many calories are in Applebee's fries?
Around 400 calories with 800mg of sodium and 50g of carbohydrate. They contain no sugar.

### Are Applebee's fries gluten-free?
They contain no wheat, but they are cooked in shared fryer oil alongside breaded chicken and fish. For coeliac disease they should be treated as cross-contaminated.

### Is Applebee's steamed broccoli vegan?
The published nutrition figures indicate butter or oil in preparation. Ordering it without butter is a standard request and makes it suitable for a plant-based meal.

### What sides come with Applebee's entrées?
It varies by dish. Burgers, sandwiches and tenders come with fries; steaks and salmon typically come with garlic mashed potatoes and broccoli; ribs and fried seafood come with fries and coleslaw.

### Does Applebee's have onion rings?
Onion rings are available at participating locations. Availability of the less common sides varies more than the core options.

### What are Applebee's pickle fries?
Fried pickle spears, served as a side or shareable. Availability varies by location.
