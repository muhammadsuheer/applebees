## What's Included in Applebee's 2 for $25

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    The 2 for $25 deal includes one full-size appetizer to share plus two full-size entrées, for $25 total. Alternatively, the appetizer can be swapped for two individual side salads. Entrées are the same portions served on the regular menu — not reduced sizes. Drinks, desserts, tax and gratuity are separate.
  </p>
</div>

The three-step structure:
1. **Pick your starter:** Any appetizer — or two side salads if you would rather start light.
2. **Each person picks an entrée:** From the rotating 2 for $25 selection.
3. **That's $25:** Exactly $12.50 per person for two full courses.

### The Side Salad Option Nobody Mentions

This is the part of the deal worth knowing about:

Instead of a shared appetizer, you can take two individual side salads — one each. It is official, it costs the same, and it changes the meal substantially.

- **Why it matters:** A shared appetizer at Applebee's runs 860 to 1,170 calories and is designed to be split. Two side salads give each person their own starter, at a fraction of that. On a deal where the entrées already clear 1,000 calories each, that is the difference between a heavy meal and a balanced one.
- **Solves the sharing compromise:** If two people want different things, or one is eating lighter, splitting a single fried appetizer is a compromise. Two salads is not.

See the [salads menu](/menu/salads) for what the side portions look like.

### Full-Size Entrées, Not Reduced Portions

The entrées on this deal are the same portions as the regular menu. This is not a value menu with smaller plates — it is the standard dish at a bundled price.

That is the core of why the deal works, and it is worth saying plainly because a lot of restaurant value menus do the opposite.

### Which Appetizer Gives You the Most

- **For protein:** The Chicken Quesadilla at 49g — more than the Boneless Wings, which most people assume is the protein pick.
- **For sharing:** The Spinach & Artichoke Dip. It is built to be split and the chips make portioning easy.
- **For flexibility:** Boneless Wings, because the sauce is chosen at the table and both people can have a say.
- **For a lighter meal:** Two side salads, every time.

## 2 for $25 Entrée Options

The entrée list rotates. Current and recurring options span burgers, chicken, pasta and salads.

### New for 2026

- **Chicken Parmesan Fettuccine:** Breaded chicken parmesan over fettuccine with marinara and melted mozzarella. Full detail on the [pasta menu](/menu/pasta).
- **Grilled Cheese Cheeseburger:** An all-beef patty served between two grilled cheese sandwiches instead of a bun. See [handcrafted burgers](/menu/handcrafted-burgers).
- **California Grilled Chicken Salad:** Grilled chicken with avocado cucumber salsa, tomatoes, bacon, Parmesan, lite Italian dressing and a balsamic glaze. The lightest of the new additions.

![Breaded chicken parmesan over fettuccine with marinara and mozzarella](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-chicken-parmesan-fettuccine.webp)

### The Recurring Classics

- **Fiesta Lime Chicken:** Grilled chicken with lime glaze, Mexi-ranch, cheddar blend, tortilla strips and Spanish rice. 1,190 calories, 61g protein. See [chicken](/menu/chicken).
- **Classic Bacon Cheeseburger:** 1,220 calories, 50g protein, the balanced order among the burgers.
- **Oriental Chicken Salad:** Applebee's signature salad. Worth knowing it is 1,560 calories with crispy chicken, which makes it heavier than the burgers rather than lighter. See [salads](/menu/salads).
- **Bourbon Street Chicken & Shrimp:** Cajun chicken and blackened shrimp on a sizzling skillet, 1,010 calories with 57g protein. See [skillets](/menu/skillets).
- **Three-Cheese Chicken Penne:** 1,350 calories with 77g of protein, the highest protein figure on the entire Applebee's menu.

### Upgrades

Premium entrées can be added for an upcharge, typically a few dollars per entrée. Which items carry one varies, so check the menu insert at your table.

If you upgrade to a steak, the 6 oz. Top Sirloin at 760 calories and 43g of protein is the leanest route. See [steaks and ribs](/menu/steaks-and-ribs).

## Is 2 for $25 Actually Worth It?

Worked with authentic Applebee's menu prices, not estimates:

Order the Spinach & Artichoke Dip ($10.49), a Classic Bacon Cheeseburger ($14.49) and Fiesta Lime Chicken ($16.49) à la carte and you are at $41.47.

- **On the deal:** $25.00.
- **Saving:** Roughly **$16.47, or about 40%**.

That holds across most combinations, because the appetizers run $9.99 to $12.99 and the entrées $14.49 to $16.49 on the regular menu. Two entrées plus an appetizer will nearly always land in the high thirties to low forties.

## How Many Calories Is the 2 for $25 Deal?

Published estimates put the full deal at 2,500 to 3,500 calories across two people — roughly 1,200 to 2,500 each depending entirely on what you pick.

That range is wide for a real reason: the menu genuinely spans both extremes.

- **The lighter route:** Two side salads instead of an appetizer, plus a California Grilled Chicken Salad and Fiesta Lime Chicken. That lands each person well under the middle of the range.
- **The heavier route:** Boneless Wings shared, plus an Oriental Chicken Salad and a Grilled Cheese Cheeseburger. The Oriental Chicken Salad alone is 1,560 calories.
- **The single biggest lever is the starter:** Swapping a 1,000-calorie shared appetizer for two side salads changes the meal more than any entrée choice.
- **The second biggest lever:** Crispy versus grilled on the chicken dishes.

Full figures: see our comprehensive [nutrition and calories](/nutrition) guide.

## Ordering 2 for $25

- **Dine-in:** The standard route, and the one where you can also stack half-price appetizers during happy hour windows.
- **Carside To Go:** The deal is available for pickup. See [takeout](/takeout).
- **Delivery:** Available through Applebee's own app and site, and through third-party platforms in most areas. Note that third-party platforms list marked-up menu prices, so ordering direct is cheaper. See [delivery](/delivery).
- **This is the opposite of half price appetizers:** The half price appetizer promotion is dine-in only. 2 for $25 travels.
- **Transit performance:** The entrées on this deal include several that do not travel well. Fried items and anything with fries soften in transit. Pasta dishes travel best.
- **Participation varies by location:** As with every Applebee's promotion, individual franchises decide. Check with your [local restaurant](/locations).

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is included in Applebee's 2 for $25?
One full-size appetizer to share plus two full-size entrées for $25. The appetizer can be swapped for two individual side salads. Drinks, desserts, tax and gratuity are separate.

### Can I get side salads instead of an appetizer on 2 for $25?
Yes. Applebee's offers two individual side salads as an alternative to the shared appetizer, at the same price. It is the lighter way to take the deal.

### What entrées are on the Applebee's 2 for $25 menu?
Current options include Chicken Parmesan Fettuccine, Grilled Cheese Cheeseburger, California Grilled Chicken Salad, Fiesta Lime Chicken, Classic Bacon Cheeseburger, Oriental Chicken Salad and Bourbon Street Chicken & Shrimp. The list rotates and varies by location.

### Are the 2 for $25 entrées full size?
Yes. They are the same portions served on the regular menu, not reduced value-menu sizes.

### Do we have to order the same entrée?
No. Each person picks their own from the 2 for $25 selection. You only need to agree on the shared appetizer — or take two side salads and skip that decision entirely.

### Are drinks included in 2 for $25?
No. Drinks, desserts, tax and gratuity are all separate. The $9.99 Really BIG Meal Deal includes a drink, which is worth factoring in when comparing them.

### Can I order 2 for $25 for takeout or delivery?
Yes. The deal is available for Carside To Go and for delivery at most locations, unlike the half price appetizer promotion, which is dine-in only.

### How much do you save with 2 for $25?
Roughly $16 on a typical combination. An appetizer plus two entrées ordered à la carte generally comes to around $38 to $42, against $25 on the deal.

### How many calories is the 2 for $25 deal?
Published estimates run 2,500 to 3,500 calories total, or roughly 1,200 to 2,500 per person. Choosing two side salads over a shared appetizer, and grilled over crispy, moves that figure the most.

### Is 2 for $25 better than the $9.99 Really BIG Meal Deal?
Two $9.99 deals cost $19.98 and include drinks and endless fries. 2 for $25 costs more but includes a starter and a much wider entrée choice. If you want an appetizer, 2 for $25 is the better structure.

### Can one person order the 2 for $25?
There is no rule against it, and the second entrée boxes up well. Pasta travels best of the options; anything fried does not.

### Can I upgrade to a steak on 2 for $25?
Premium entrées are available for an upcharge of a few dollars. Which items carry one varies by location, so check the menu insert at your table.
