## Which Applebee's Burger to Order

**The best value isn't on this table.** It's the BIG Classic Bacon Cheeseburger on the [Really BIG Meal Deal](/menu/really-big-meal-deal): a larger build than the $14.49 Classic Bacon Cheeseburger, with fries and a fountain drink, for $9.99. If a bacon cheeseburger is what you want, order it that way.

**Off the regular menu, the Classic Bacon Cheeseburger wins.** It's the cheapest burger at $14.49, has 50g of protein and the least sodium of the burgers that list it, at 2,640mg.

**For the most protein, the Whisky Bacon Burger.** 63g. It also has 97g of fat and 32g of saturated fat, the most of any burger.

**Don't pick the Impossible for sodium.** It's the lightest burger and has 12g of fiber, more than any other dish here, and just 35mg of cholesterol against the beef Classic's 120mg. But its 2,910mg of sodium is higher than the beef burger's.

## Making an Applebee's Burger Lighter

The side moves the number more than the burger does. Classic Fries are 400 calories. Swap them for Steamed Broccoli (100) and you've cut 300 calories from any burger. Ask for Mexi-ranch or the whisky glaze on the side and you control the rest. Skipping the bun takes out most of the carbohydrate, if your restaurant will do it. See [sides](/menu/sides) for every swap.

## Do Applebee's Burgers Travel Well?

Better than fried appetizers, worse than pasta. The bun soaks up steam from the patty in a closed box, and the fries go soft first. For [takeout](/takeout), swap fries for mashed potatoes or steamed vegetables, which arrive in the shape they left. Remember that the O-M-Cheese-adilla Burger and O-M-Cheese Burger aren't available to go at all.

## What to Know Before You Order a Burger

**The Grilled Cheese Cheeseburger is the one to try.** Four slices of American on toasted potato bread, a sweet and savory jam, and a cup of tomato basil soup for dipping. It's the most fun burger on the menu.

**The Quesadilla Burger is great, and messy.** Toasty cheese-filled tortillas take the place of a bun, with pico and Mexi-ranch cutting through the richness. It's greasy and can fall apart halfway through, so grab extra napkins before it lands.

**The Whisky Bacon Burger is for sweet and smoky fans.** The sauce leans sweet and tangy, and the smoked bacon keeps it from tasting one-note.

**Eat the O-M-Cheese Burger while it's sizzling.** The skillet cheese is gooey when it arrives and firms up as it cools, so don't let it sit.

**If you order a classic, make it the bacon one.** The Classic Bacon Cheeseburger is the best of the basic burgers because the bacon adds real crunch. For a burger you'll remember, pick one of the specialty burgers instead.
