## The Lightest Entrée at Applebee's

If you want a full dinner at Applebee's under 750 calories, this is the only entrée that gets you there.

The Tex-Mex Shrimp Bowl, at 710, beats every other entrée on the menu. The next lightest are the 6 oz. Top Sirloin, the Grilled Chicken Breast and a half rack of Baby Back Ribs, all at 760. It's lighter than the Blackened Cajun Salmon (850) and every entrée salad, the lightest of which are 900. The Southwest Chicken Bowl, at 820, is still lighter than every burger, every pasta and every entrée salad.

The trade-off is that there's less hiding in a bowl. You won't get fries or a bun, and the bowl is the meal. If that sounds like a feature, order it.

## What's in an Irresist-A-Bowl

Both bowls are built the same way, which is why they eat lighter than the plates around them:

- **At the bottom:** cilantro rice and fresh greens.
- **In the middle:** house-made pico de gallo, black bean corn salsa and guacamole.
- **On top:** chipotle lime chicken or shrimp, a drizzle of chimichurri, crispy tortilla strips and a lime wedge.

Chimichurri is a herb and garlic sauce, not a creamy dressing, and that's a big part of why the calories stay down. Chipotle is smoked jalapeño, so the heat is smoky and warm, not sharp, and the lime and guacamole cool it further.

## Making a Bowl Your Own

Because the bowls are layered instead of cooked together, they're easy to adjust. Ask for the tortilla strips on the side if you're watching carbs or taking it home. Chimichurri on the side gives you control over the dressing. And ask whether your restaurant will make it with extra greens instead of rice, which cuts most of the starch.

## Allergens

The Tex-Mex Shrimp Bowl contains shellfish, and shared grills mean the chicken bowl isn't safe for a shellfish allergy either. The tortilla strips contain wheat. We don't have full allergen data for the bowls, so for any allergy, talk to the kitchen. The [allergen menu](/allergen-menu) covers the items we track.

## Bowls To Go

Bowls travel better than almost anything else on the menu, because nothing in them depends on staying crispy except the tortilla strips. Get those and the chimichurri on the side. Eat it soon: guacamole browns as it sits. Don't microwave the whole bowl, or the greens and guacamole go warm and limp. More on [Carside To Go](/takeout).
