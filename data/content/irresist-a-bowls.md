## What's in an Applebee's Irresist-A-Bowl

Each bowl layers chipotle lime chicken or shrimp over cilantro rice and fresh greens, with house-made pico de gallo, black bean corn salsa and guacamole, finished with chimichurri, crispy tortilla strips and a fresh lime wedge.

Three things separate these from the rest of the menu:

- **The only entrées with guacamole:** Nothing else on the Applebee's menu includes guacamole as a standard ingredient, making the bowls the one place you get avocado without paying an upcharge.
- **The lightest full entrées Applebee's serves:** At 710 and 820 calories, they sit well below a menu where most mains clear 1,000 calories  achieved through grilling and fresh produce rather than reduced portion sizes.
- **The lime wedge is served on the side:** Squeezing fresh lime at the table delivers bright, immediate acidity rather than muted acidity that has sat in a sauce.

![Layered bowls with rice, greens, salsa and grilled protein](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-irresist-a-bowls-menu-category.webp)

### How the Bowl is Layered

1. **Rice and greens at the base:** Fluffy cilantro rice provides an aromatic foundation that absorbs drippings from above; fresh greens keep the base light and refreshing.
2. **Salsas and guacamole in the middle:** House-made pico de gallo supplies crisp acidity, black bean corn salsa adds texture and sweetness, and rich guacamole provides the healthy fats that carry the warm chipotle heat.
3. **Protein and chimichurri on top:** Grilled protein rests on top, drizzled with chimichurri  an herb-and-garlic sauce rather than a heavy creamy dressing, preserving the light calorie profile.
4. **Tortilla strips last:** Added on top so they remain crisp against the warm and cool ingredients underneath.

### Southwest Chicken Bowl

![Grilled chipotle lime chicken over cilantro rice with salsa, guacamole and tortilla strips](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-southwest-chicken-bowl-entree.webp)

The signature bowl and the staple of Applebee's bowl lineup:

Grilled chipotle lime chicken breast served over fresh mixed greens and cilantro rice, accompanied by house-made pico de gallo, black bean corn salsa and fresh guacamole. Finished with chimichurri sauce, crispy tortilla strips and a fresh lime wedge.

- **Chipotle lime marinade:** Chipotle is smoked jalapeño, providing deep smoky warmth rather than sharp burning heat, tempered by fresh lime. It is the same marinade featured in the [Chicken Quesadilla](/menu/appetizers) and Chicken Fajita Rollup.
- **Calorie comparison:** At 820 calories, it delivers a complete, satisfying dinner below every burger, every pasta dish and most salads on the menu.
- **Pricing:** $14.99 · 820 calories.

### Tex-Mex Shrimp Bowl

![Grilled shrimp over cilantro rice with greens, salsa and guacamole](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-tex-mex-shrimp-bowl-entree.webp)

The lightest full entrée on the entire Applebee's menu at just 710 calories:

Tender grilled chipotle lime shrimp layered over cilantro rice and fresh greens with house-made pico de gallo, black bean corn salsa, guacamole, chimichurri and tortilla strips.

- **Why it is so light:** Shrimp is naturally lean and low in fat while absorbing the bold chipotle lime marinade completely.
- **Menu-wide comparison:** The [8 oz. Top Sirloin](/menu/steaks-and-ribs) is 830 calories, the [Blackened Cajun Salmon](/menu/seafood) is 850 calories, and the [Grilled Chicken Caesar](/menu/salads) is 950 calories. This bowl is lighter than every single one of them.
- **Pricing:** $15.99 · 710 calories · contains shellfish.

### Rotating Third Bowl

Applebee's periodically rotates a third bowl through seasonal menus. Past limited-time variations have included a homestyle crispy chicken bowl with mashed potatoes and gravy, and a Broccoli Cheddar Mac 'N Cheese Bowl. Check your local restaurant for current regional availability.

## Customising Your Irresist-A-Bowl

Because bowl ingredients are layered rather than cooked into a single casserole, these are among the most modifiable entrées at Applebee's:

- **Add sliced steak:** Add grilled sirloin steak for extra protein  see [steaks and ribs](/menu/steaks-and-ribs).
- **Hold the rice, double the greens:** Turns the bowl into a massive loaded salad and cuts out the majority of carbohydrates.
- **Skip the tortilla strips:** Saves refined carbohydrates and is recommended on [takeout](/takeout) orders where strips may soften in transit.
- **Chimichurri on the side:** Gives you total control over dressing portions, though chimichurri is an olive-oil-and-herb sauce rather than a calorie-heavy cream.
- **Order meatless for a complete vegetarian entrée:** Without meat, the bowl still offers cilantro rice, greens, black beans, corn, pico de gallo and guacamole. Review [nutrition and allergens](/nutrition) for dietary specifics.
- **Extra guacamole:** The most popular upgrade  guacamole carries the smoky chipotle heat and provides rich creaminess.

## Dietary and Nutritional Overview

- **Lightest entrée on the menu:** Tex-Mex Shrimp Bowl at 710 calories.
- **Guacamole inclusion:** Standard in both bowls; avocado appears nowhere else as an included ingredient.
- **Shellfish allergy:** Present in the Tex-Mex Shrimp Bowl. Shared cooking surfaces mean cross-contact is possible even when ordering the chicken bowl.
- **Vegetarian dining:** Ordering either bowl without protein yields a hearty meal of rice, beans, corn, greens, pico and guacamole.
- **Low-carb option:** Holding the rice and doubling greens eliminates the primary starch.
- **Gluten:** Tortilla strips contain wheat flour; request them omitted if gluten-sensitive.

## Do the Bowls Travel Well?

Irresist-A-Bowls travel better for takeout and delivery than almost any other item on the menu:

- **Layered vs. assembled:** Unlike burgers where buns absorb moisture or pasta where sauce congeals, bowl components sit stably alongside each other.
- **Key takeout requests:** Ask for tortilla strips on the side so they stay crisp, and chimichurri on the side to prevent greens from wilting.
- **Guacamole freshness:** Fresh avocado oxidizes and browns over time  eat within 20 to 30 minutes for optimal taste and appearance.
- **Temperature balance:** These bowls are designed to be warm (rice and protein) and cool (greens, pico, guacamole). Reheating the entire bowl in a microwave wilts the greens and warms the guacamole.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What are Applebee's Irresist-A-Bowls?
Layered bowls built on cilantro rice and fresh greens with chipotle lime chicken or shrimp, house-made pico de gallo, black bean corn salsa and guacamole, topped with chimichurri, tortilla strips and a lime wedge.

### What is in the Applebee's Southwest Chicken Bowl?
Grilled chipotle lime chicken on fresh greens and cilantro rice with house-made pico de gallo, black bean corn salsa and guacamole, topped with chimichurri, crispy tortilla strips and a fresh lime wedge. It is 820 calories.

### Which Applebee's entrée has the fewest calories?
The Tex-Mex Shrimp Bowl at 710 calories  lighter than the 6 oz. Top Sirloin at 760, the Blackened Cajun Salmon at 850 and every salad on the menu.

### Do the bowls come with guacamole?
Yes. Both bowls include guacamole as a standard component, and they are effectively the only place it appears on the Applebee's menu.

### Are the bowls served hot or cold?
Both. The cilantro rice and the protein are served hot; the greens, pico de gallo and salsas are cold. That contrast is the point of the format.

### Can I add steak to an Applebee's bowl?
Yes. Sliced grilled steak is available as an add-on for an upcharge, in place of or alongside the chicken or shrimp.

### Can I get a bowl without rice?
Yes. Holding the rice and doubling the greens turns the bowl into a large loaded salad and removes most of the carbohydrate. It is the biggest single change available on these items.

### Is the Southwest Chicken Bowl spicy?
Mildly. Chipotle is smoked jalapeño, so it brings smoke as much as heat, and the lime and guacamole temper it. It is closer to warm than hot.

### Are Applebee's bowls vegetarian?
Ordered without the protein, the Southwest bowl is a complete vegetarian meal  rice, greens, black beans, corn, pico de gallo and guacamole. Check the rice and chimichurri if you are eating strictly plant-based.

### How much are Applebee's Irresist-A-Bowls?
Around $14.99 to $15.99. Prices vary by location, since Applebee's restaurants are independently franchised.

### Are the bowls on the 2 for $25 menu?
Bowls appear on the 2 for $25 entrée list at participating locations, though the selection rotates. Combined with taking two side salads instead of the shared appetizer, it produces the lightest version of that deal.

### Do Irresist-A-Bowls travel well for takeout?
Yes, better than most of the menu, because the components sit alongside each other rather than being assembled. Ask for tortilla strips and chimichurri on the side, and eat within about twenty minutes since guacamole browns.
