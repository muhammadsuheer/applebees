## Fresh, Crisp, and Craveable: Applebee's Salads

When it comes to casual dining, the salad section is often viewed as the "compromise" menuthe place you look when you feel guilty about ordering a burger. But at Applebee's, the **Salads** category defies this stereotype entirely. Rather than serving sad, wilted greens with a drizzle of afterthought dressing, Applebee's has engineered salads that are massive, craveable, and often the most popular items ordered at any given table.

Applebee's understands that a great salad must be a complete meal, offering a variety of textures, robust proteins, and signature dressings that you simply cannot replicate at home. In this guide, we will explore the absolute powerhouse that is the Oriental Chicken Salad, break down the execution of their classic Caesar, and discuss why these bowls of greens command so much respect.

---

### The Icon: The Oriental Chicken Salad

![A high-end food photography shot of a massive Oriental Chicken Salad with sweet vinaigrette pouring over the top.](/apple-bees-menu/salads-lifestyle.jpg)

![Applebee's Oriental Chicken Salad Bowl](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-oriental-chicken-salad-bowl.webp)

If there is a Mount Rushmore of Applebee's menu items, the **Oriental Chicken Salad** is firmly carved into the rock alongside the Spinach & Artichoke Dip and the Fiesta Lime Chicken. This salad has maintained an incredible cult following for decades, and for very good reason: it is a masterpiece of texture and flavor.

#### The Greens and Crunch
The foundation of the salad is a bed of crisp, fresh Asian greens and cabbage. The cabbage is crucial here, as it provides a sturdy crunch that doesn't immediately wilt when dressed. Tossed in with the greens are toasted sliced almonds and crispy chow mein noodles, introducing a nutty, savory crunch that makes every bite interesting.

#### The Chicken
Diners have the option of choosing either crispy breaded chicken tenders or grilled chicken breast for this salad. The traditional and most popular choice is the crispy chicken. The warm, heavily breaded, savory chicken provides a beautiful temperature and texture contrast against the cold, crisp greens. 

#### The Signature Vinaigrette
The true star of the show, however, is the **Oriental Vinaigrette**. This dressing is so beloved that copycat recipes have flooded the internet for years. It is a brilliant emulsion of sweet honey, tangy rice vinegar, savory soy sauce, and a hint of sesame oil. The vinaigrette is thick enough to coat every leaf and noodle, tying the rich chicken and the fresh greens together in a sweet-and-savory embrace. 

At 1420 calories (with crispy chicken and full dressing), it is a very hearty, filling meal. For those looking to lighten the load, ordering the salad with grilled chicken and the dressing on the side is a fantastic way to enjoy the iconic flavors with fewer calories.

### The Timeless Standard: Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad

![Applebee's Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-grilled-chicken-caesar-salad.webp)

While the Oriental Chicken Salad represents Applebee's innovative side, the **Grilled Chicken Caesar Salad** is a testament to their ability to execute a timeless classic flawlessly. 

#### The Anatomy of a Great Caesar
A Caesar salad is deceptively simple, meaning there is nowhere to hide if the ingredients are subpar. Applebee's starts with fresh, crisp romaine lettuce. The lettuce is tossed in a rich, creamy, garlic-forward Caesar dressing that offers the perfect balance of salty parmesan and tangy lemon. 

To provide the necessary crunch, the salad is topped with large, garlic-herb croutons that hold up well to the heavy dressing without turning to mush.

#### The Protein 
The salad is crowned with a generously sized, hot grilled chicken breast. The chicken is seasoned simply and bears the distinct, smoky char marks from the grill. This smoky, savory protein turns a simple side salad into a robust, 820-calorie dinner that satisfies the appetite without inducing a food coma.

### Why Applebee's Salads Work

The success of Applebee's salad menu boils down to three core principles:

1. **Portion Size:** These are not side salads. They are served in massive, wide bowls that allow for proper tossing and ensure you won't leave the restaurant hungry.
2. **Temperature Contrast:** The combination of ice-cold, crisp greens with hot, freshly cooked proteins (like crispy tenders or grilled chicken) elevates the dining experience. It prevents the dish from feeling monotonous.
3. **Proprietary Dressings:** Applebee's does not rely on generic, bottled dressings. Their signature vinaigrettes and ranches are crafted specifically to pair perfectly with the ingredients in the bowl.

---

### Customizing Your Greens

One of the best aspects of ordering a salad at Applebee's is the extreme flexibility. 
- **Protein Swaps:** You can easily swap the chicken on any salad for blackened shrimp or even a sliced sirloin steak for an upcharge. 
- **Dressing Control:** Always ask for dressing on the side if you want to control your caloric intake or prevent your salad from becoming soggy if you plan to take half of it home.
- **Mix and Match:** Don't be afraid to add extra toppings. Adding a handful of bacon bits or extra cheese to a Caesar salad can take it to the next level.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Can I buy the Oriental Vinaigrette in stores?**
Unfortunately, Applebee's does not currently sell their proprietary Oriental Vinaigrette in grocery stores. The only way to get the authentic flavor is to visit the restaurant (or order an extra side of it to go!).

**Are the salads prepared fresh?**
Yes. The lettuce and greens are chopped and prepared fresh daily in the Applebee's kitchen to ensure maximum crispness.

**Is the Oriental Chicken Salad spicy?**
Not at all. The flavor profile of the Oriental Chicken Salad is sweet, savory, and slightly nutty. There is no heat involved in the dressing or the chicken preparation.
