## What's on Applebee's Seafood Menu

Seafood is the smallest section of the Applebee's menu and the one with the widest internal range. That gap is the thing worth understanding before ordering:

- **It contains the menu's heaviest item.** Hand-Battered Fish & Chips at 1,680 calories is the highest-calorie single order Applebee's serves — higher than any burger, steak or pasta dish.
- **It also contains one of the lightest entrées.** Blackened Cajun Salmon is flagged as a low-calorie option in Applebee's own nutrition data, at 850 calories with 44g of protein.
- **The difference is entirely the batter.** Both are fish. One is blackened on a hot surface with spices; the other is battered and fried. That single preparation choice accounts for 830 calories and 62g of fat.
- **Salmon is the only omega-3 source on the menu.** Nothing else Applebee's serves offers them. For anyone choosing seafood for health reasons rather than preference, that is the actual differentiator — not the calorie count.

### Reading the table

- **The salmon carries the lowest sodium in the category at 2,050mg** — well below the fish and chips at 3,500mg, and among the lower figures across the whole menu.
- **Protein is almost identical between the salmon and the fish and chips** — 44g against 43g. The batter adds 830 calories and 62g of fat without adding protein. Put plainly: you are paying 830 calories for the coating.
- **Fat is where the gap is widest:** 117g in the fish and chips against 55g in the salmon. That is the largest fat figure on the Applebee's menu.
- **The salmon is the better nutritional order by every measure here** — fewer calories, less than half the fat, less sodium, slightly more protein, plus omega-3s the fried options do not carry. The fish and chips wins on one thing only, and it is not a metric that appears in a table.

## Detailed Seafood Dish Breakdowns

### Hand-Battered Fish & Chips

![Hand-battered white fish fillets fried with seasoned fries and tartar sauce](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-hand-battered-fish-and-chips.webp)

The most-searched item in this category, and the heaviest single order on the Applebee's menu.

White fish in a hand-battered coating, fried and served with fries. The batter is the point — hand-battered means coated to order rather than arriving pre-breaded, which is what produces the thicker, more irregular crust.

**On the numbers:** 1,680 calories with 117g of fat and 3,500mg of sodium. It carries 43g of protein and 11g of fiber, and 22g of sugar, most of which comes from the batter and accompanying sauces rather than the fish.

This is a full indulgent meal rather than a light seafood plate, and worth ordering knowing that. Swapping the fries for [steamed vegetables](/menu/sides) is the change that moves the figures most.

- **Price & Nutrition:** 1,680 calories · 43g protein · contains fish, wheat, soy and milk

### Blackened Cajun Salmon

![Blackened salmon fillet with mashed potatoes and broccoli](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-blackened-cajun-salmon-fillet.webp)

The only omega-3 source on the Applebee's menu, and the strongest nutritional order in this category.

A salmon fillet coated in Cajun seasoning — paprika, garlic, onion, thyme, oregano and cayenne — and seared on a very hot surface. The high heat sets the spices into a dark crust almost immediately, which is what "blackened" describes. That crust seals moisture in, which is why blackening suits salmon better than baking does.

Served with garlic mashed potatoes and broccoli.

**On the numbers:** 850 calories with 44g of protein and 2,050mg of sodium — the lowest sodium in the category. Applebee's flags it as both a low-calorie and pescatarian option in its own nutrition data.

The omega-3 point matters more than the calorie count. Salmon is the only item on this menu that provides them, which makes this dish genuinely different rather than just lighter.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $18.99 · 850 calories · 44g protein · contains fish, milk and soy

### Double Crunch Shrimp

![Battered fried shrimp with cocktail sauce and fries](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-double-crunch-shrimp-platter.webp)

The fried option for people who want shellfish rather than fish.

Battered shrimp fried until the coating shatters — "double crunch" describes the texture of the breading rather than a cooking step. Served with cocktail sauce, fries and coleslaw.

The cocktail sauce is doing more work than it appears to. Horseradish cuts fried richness better than a creamy dip would, which is why it is the standard pairing for fried shellfish everywhere.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $15.99 · 1,210 calories · contains shellfish

For grilled shrimp instead, the [Bourbon Street Chicken & Shrimp](/menu/skillets) skillet uses blackened shrimp rather than battered — the same protein without the coating.

## Where to Get Fish and Chips Near You

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    Applebee's serves hand-battered fish and chips at participating locations. Seafood availability is more variable than core menu items — some locations carry the full seafood range year-round, others rotate it seasonally.
  </p>
</div>

**Why this varies more than other categories:** Seafood carries higher and less predictable ingredient costs than beef or chicken, so franchise owners have more discretion over what they stock. A location may carry salmon but not fish and chips, or run seafood as a seasonal feature.

**How to check before you go:** The [location finder](/locations) shows nearby restaurants with contact details, and calling ahead is worth it for seafood specifically — more so than for burgers or chicken, where availability is near-universal.

If your local restaurant does not carry fish and chips, the Double Crunch Shrimp is the closest fried seafood alternative and is more consistently stocked.

Hours vary by restaurant — see [operating hours](/operating-hours).

## Building a Surf and Turf at Applebee's

Applebee's does not list a surf and turf on the menu, but the components exist.

Adding a shrimp portion to a steak from the [steaks and ribs](/menu/steaks-and-ribs) menu produces one. The 8 oz. Top Sirloin at 830 calories with 55g of protein is the usual base — it is the leanest steak on the menu, which leaves room for the addition.

Blackened shrimp rather than battered keeps the addition light. The battered version adds substantially more.

Availability of shrimp as an add-on varies by location — worth asking rather than assuming.

## Dietary Notes

- **Pescatarian:** The Blackened Cajun Salmon is flagged as pescatarian in Applebee's nutrition data.
- **Omega-3:** Salmon only. No other item on the Applebee's menu provides them.
- **Lowest sodium in the category:** Blackened Cajun Salmon at 2,050mg.
- **Allergens:** The salmon contains fish, milk and soy. The fish and chips contains fish, wheat, soy and milk. The shrimp contains shellfish.
- **Cross-contact warning:** Fried seafood shares fryer oil with breaded chicken and other items. Anyone with a shellfish allergy should note that ordering fish rather than shrimp does not remove the risk when both come out of the same fryer. See [nutrition and allergens](/nutrition).
- **Gluten:** Both fried items contain wheat. The blackened salmon does not carry a wheat flag, making it the option for anyone avoiding gluten — though Applebee's does not run a certified gluten-free kitchen.

## Does Applebee's Seafood Travel Well?

Split answer, and it is worth knowing which side your order falls on:

- **The salmon travels well:** No coating to lose, and the blackened crust holds up. It reheats reasonably in a pan.
- **Fried seafood is the worst travelling category on the menu:** Battered fish and shrimp lose texture faster than anything else — the coating is thicker and holds more steam than breaded chicken does. Twenty minutes in a closed container softens it substantially.
- **If ordering fried seafood to go:** Ask for cocktail sauce and dips on the side, eat it first, and reheat in an air fryer rather than a microwave. Roughly four to six minutes at 375°F restores most of the crust.
- **Shrimp overcooks faster than fish when reheated:** If a meal is being split across two sittings, the shrimp goes in the first.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Does Applebee's have fish and chips?
Yes. Hand-Battered Fish & Chips is served at participating locations — white fish in a hand-battered coating with fries. At 1,680 calories it is the highest-calorie single item on the Applebee's menu. Availability varies more than core menu items.

### What seafood does Applebee's have?
Hand-Battered Fish & Chips, Blackened Cajun Salmon and Double Crunch Shrimp. Blackened shrimp also appears in the Bourbon Street Chicken & Shrimp skillet.

### How many calories are in Applebee's salmon?
Blackened Cajun Salmon is 850 calories with 44g of protein and 2,050mg of sodium — the lowest sodium of any entrée in this category. Applebee's flags it as a low-calorie and pescatarian option.

### Is Applebee's salmon healthy?
It is the strongest nutritional order in this category: 44g of protein, the lowest sodium of the seafood dishes, and the only source of omega-3 fatty acids on the Applebee's menu.

### Which Applebee's seafood dish is lightest?
Blackened Cajun Salmon at 850 calories. The fried options are considerably heavier — Double Crunch Shrimp at 1,210 and Hand-Battered Fish & Chips at 1,680.

### Can I get grilled shrimp at Applebee's instead of fried?
Blackened shrimp appears in the Bourbon Street Chicken & Shrimp skillet rather than as a standalone grilled dish. Whether it can be substituted or added elsewhere varies by location.

### Is the Blackened Cajun Salmon spicy?
The Cajun seasoning is smoky and savoury with mild to moderate heat rather than sharp spice. The richness of the salmon balances it.

### How much is seafood at Applebee's?
The Blackened Cajun Salmon is $18.99 and the Double Crunch Shrimp $15.99. Prices vary by location, since restaurants are independently franchised.

### Does Applebee's seafood contain gluten?
The battered fish and shrimp contain wheat. The blackened salmon does not carry a wheat flag. Applebee's does not operate a certified gluten-free kitchen, and shared fryers create cross-contact risk.

### Can I make a surf and turf at Applebee's?
There is no surf and turf on the menu, but adding a shrimp portion to a steak achieves it. The 8 oz. Top Sirloin is the usual base at 830 calories with 55g of protein. Availability of shrimp as an add-on varies by location.
