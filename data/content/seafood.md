## Catch of the Day: Exploring Applebee's Seafood Selection

When diners walk through the doors of a neighborhood grill and bar, their minds typically drift toward towering burgers or sizzling steaks. Seafood is often viewed as an afterthoughta token gesture for pescatarians. However, at Applebee's, the **Seafood** category is anything but an afterthought. It is a carefully curated selection of high-quality proteins designed to offer a lighter, more refreshing, yet equally satisfying alternative to heavy red meat.

Applebee's approaches seafood with the same culinary philosophy they apply to the rest of their menu: big, bold flavors, generous portions, and absolute consistency. Whether you are craving a guilt-free, heart-healthy dinner or a decadent, golden-fried feast, the seafood offerings deliver on all fronts. In this deep dive, we will explore why you should consider ordering the catch of the day on your next visit.

### Why Choose Seafood at a Neighborhood Grill?

In recent years, consumer eating habits have shifted significantly. Diners are becoming more conscious of what they put into their bodies, seeking out meals that provide sustained energy without the heavy, lethargic feeling that often follows a massive burger or a heavy pasta dish.

Seafood answers this call perfectly. It is naturally lean, packed with high-quality protein, and in the case of salmon, loaded with essential Omega-3 fatty acids. By offering expertly prepared seafood options, Applebee's ensures that a group of friends with vastly different dietary goals can still comfortably dine together. You can enjoy a nutrient-dense, calorie-conscious salmon fillet while your friend across the table tackles a rack of ribs.

But beyond the health benefits, seafood at Applebee's is simply delicious. They rely on robust spice blends, high-heat cooking techniques, and perfect batter consistencies to make their aquatic offerings shine.

### Spotlight on Flavor: Blackened Cajun Salmon

![A high-end food photography shot of a perfectly seared blackened cajun salmon fillet with broccoli and mashed potatoes.](/apple-bees-menu/seafood-lifestyle.jpg)

![Applebee's Blackened Cajun Salmon Fillet](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-blackened-cajun-salmon-fillet.webp)

For diners seeking the ultimate intersection of health and flavor, the **Blackened Cajun Salmon** is arguably the best item on the entire Applebee's menu. This dish proves that eating well does not have to mean sacrificing taste.

#### The Sourcing and Preparation
Applebee's utilizes a premium salmon fillet that is prized for its firm texture and rich, buttery flavor. Rather than simply baking the fish, which can result in a bland and watery dish, the chefs employ a classic Southern cooking technique: blackening. 

The salmon is heavily coated in a proprietary blend of Cajun spices. This blend typically includes paprika, garlic powder, onion powder, thyme, oregano, and cayenne pepper. 

#### The Cooking Process
The seasoned fillet is dropped onto a screaming-hot flat top grill or cast-iron surface. The intense heat immediately sears the spices into the flesh of the fish, creating a dark, crusty, highly flavorful exteriorthis is the "blackened" crust. The crust seals in the natural moisture of the salmon, ensuring the interior remains tender and flaky, never dry.

#### Nutritional Profile and Pairings
At an incredibly lean **640 calories**, the Blackened Cajun Salmon is a nutritional powerhouse. It delivers a massive dose of protein and healthy fats while remaining remarkably light. It is traditionally served with a side of steamed broccoli and garlic mashed potatoes. The subtle sweetness of the salmon and the spicy kick of the Cajun seasoning pair brilliantly with a crisp, dry white wine like a Sauvignon Blanc or a light, citrusy wheat beer.

### The Indulgent Crunch: Double Crunch Shrimp

![Applebee's Double Crunch Shrimp Platter](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-double-crunch-shrimp-platter.webp)

If the salmon represents restraint and health consciousness, the **Double Crunch Shrimp** represents pure, unadulterated fried joy. This dish is for the diner who wants the satisfying, savory crunch of a deep-fried meal but prefers the delicate flavor of shellfish over chicken.

#### The Secret is in the Breading
Frying shrimp is a delicate balancing act. If the batter is too thin, it falls off in the fryer; if it is too thick, it overpowers the delicate meat inside. Applebee's solves this problem with their "Double Crunch" breading. 

The shrimp are coated in a specialized, craggy breadcrumb mixture that fries to a beautiful golden brown. The term "double crunch" is not just marketing jargonthe breading truly shatters when you bite into it, providing a deeply satisfying textural experience. Despite the heavy breading, the shrimp inside remain plump, sweet, and snappy. 

#### The Ultimate Dipping Experience
No fried seafood platter is complete without a great sauce, and this dish is served with a generous side of classic cocktail sauce. The sharp, pungent bite of the horseradish in the cocktail sauce cuts straight through the richness of the fried breading, balancing every bite. 

Served alongside a mountain of classic French fries and a side of creamy coleslaw, the Double Crunch Shrimp platter is a hearty, 1210-calorie meal that hits every nostalgic comfort food button.

### Creating the Ultimate "Surf and Turf"

One of the best-kept secrets of the Applebee's menu is the ability to create your own custom "Surf and Turf" experience. You don't have to choose between the land and the sea!

If you are ordering an 8 oz. Top Sirloin or a Bourbon Street Steak, you can ask your server to add a side portion of the Double Crunch Shrimp to your plate. This transforms a standard steak dinner into a premium, celebratory feast. The contrast between the rich, beefy steak and the crispy, sweet shrimp is a flavor combination that has stood the test of time in the culinary world.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Is the Blackened Cajun Salmon very spicy?**
The Cajun seasoning provides a robust, smoky flavor with a mild-to-moderate level of heat. It is not designed to be painfully spicy. The heat is balanced nicely by the natural richness of the salmon.

**Can I get the shrimp grilled instead of fried?**
Yes! If you want the protein of the shrimp without the heavy calories of the "Double Crunch" breading, you can often request a skewer of grilled, blackened shrimp (the same shrimp used in the Bourbon Street Chicken & Shrimp skillet) as a substitute or a side addition.

**What is the best side dish for the salmon?**
To keep the meal light and healthy, steamed broccoli is the perfect pairing. The crisp, green vegetable provides great texture and color contrast to the dark, blackened crust of the fish.
