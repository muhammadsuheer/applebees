## Applebee's Steaks & Ribs: A Masterclass in Wood-Fired Flavor

When you think of casual dining, the standard fare of burgers, chicken tenders, and salads often comes to mind. But Applebee's has spent decades perfecting something far more ambitious: a robust, high-quality lineup of **Steaks & Ribs** that rivals dedicated steakhouses in both flavor and value. Whether you are celebrating a special occasion or simply craving a hearty, protein-packed dinner, the Steaks & Ribs menu at Applebee's delivers an exceptional dining experience.

This category is built on the foundation of premium cuts of meat, expertly applied marinades, and the unmistakable, smoky char that can only be achieved on a roaring hot grill. In this comprehensive guide, we will explore the specific cuts offered by Applebee's, dive into their legendary Applewood Smoked Ribs, and provide tips on how to order your steak exactly the way you like it.

### The Art of the Applebee's Steak

![A highly detailed shot of a grilled ribeye steak and a half rack of BBQ ribs in a steakhouse setting.](/apple-bees-menu/steaks-and-ribs-lifestyle.jpg)

Cooking a great steak is both an art and a science, and it starts long before the meat hits the grill. Applebee's sources high-quality beef that is aged to ensure maximum tenderness and flavor development. The chefs utilize a combination of dry seasoning and high-heat grilling to create a beautiful, caramelized crust on the outside while keeping the inside tender and juicy.

#### The 8 oz. Top Sirloin

![Applebee's 8oz Top Sirloin Steak Dinner](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-8oz-top-sirloin-steak-dinner.webp)

For diners looking for a lean, protein-packed meal that doesn't compromise on flavor, the **8 oz. Top Sirloin** is the go-to choice. Sirloin is a naturally leaner cut of beef, meaning it has less intramuscular fat (marbling) than a ribeye. 

Because it is leaner, the sirloin relies heavily on the quality of the grill to impart flavor. Applebee's seasons their sirloin with a classic salt and pepper blend and grills it over an open flame. This creates a slightly smoky, charred exterior that contrasts beautifully with the firm, beefy chew of the interior. 

At only 630 calories (before sides), it is a fantastic option for athletes, those on a high-protein diet, or anyone looking for a lighter dinner that still feels substantial. It pairs wonderfully with a side of steamed broccoli and a baked potato.

#### The 12 oz. Ribeye

![Applebee's Ribeye Steak Dinner](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-ribeye-steak-dinner.webp)

On the opposite end of the spectrum is the decadent **12 oz. Ribeye**. The ribeye is universally beloved by steak aficionados for one primary reason: fat. The heavy marbling running throughout the cut melts during the cooking process, basting the steak from the inside out. 

This results in a steak that is incredibly juicy, tender, and intensely flavorful. The fat also helps the exterior crisp up beautifully on the grill, creating a savory crust that is completely irresistible. Because the ribeye is so rich, it pairs best with bold, starchy sides like the Garlic Mashed Potatoes or the Loaded Baked Potato, which can help soak up the delicious juices left on the plate.

#### The Bourbon Street Steak

![Applebee's Bourbon Street Steak Dinner](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-bourbon-street-steak-dinner.webp)

Taking inspiration from the flavors of New Orleans, the **Bourbon Street Steak** is a culinary adventure. Applebee's takes a high-quality steak and glazes it with a sweet, savory, and slightly smoky bourbon sauce. 

But the glaze is only half the story. The steak is served covered in a generous portion of sautéed mushrooms and deeply caramelized onions. The earthy mushrooms and sweet onions play perfectly against the sharp, savory notes of the bourbon glaze, creating a deeply complex flavor profile. This dish offers the robust satisfaction of a steakhouse dinner with the bold, unapologetic flavors Applebee's is famous for.

### The Mastery of Applewood Smoked Ribs

![Applebee's Applewood Smoked Baby Back Ribs](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-applewood-smoked-baby-back-ribs.webp)

While the steaks are exceptional, no discussion of this menu category is complete without mentioning the legendary **Applewood Smoked Ribs**. Applebee's has spent years refining their rib recipe, and the result is a rack of ribs that can stand toe-to-toe with dedicated BBQ joints.

#### The Smoking Process
Great ribs require time and patience. Applebee's ribs are slow-cooked for hours before they ever see a grill. This slow-cooking process breaks down the tough connective tissues in the pork, resulting in meat that is incredibly tender and truly "fall-off-the-bone."

The ribs are smoked using real Applewood, which imparts a slightly sweet, mild, and fruity smoke flavor that doesn't overpower the natural taste of the pork. 

#### The Finish
Once the ribs are perfectly tender, they are moved to the high-heat grill. Here, they are aggressively slathered with Applebee's signature BBQ sauce. The sugar in the sauce caramelizes quickly on the grill, creating sticky, sweet, and slightly charred edges that are the hallmark of great barbecue. 

Served with a side of classic crispy fries and a side of creamy coleslaw (to cut through the richness of the meat), the Applewood Smoked Ribs are a messy, satisfying, and utterly delicious dining experience. At 1400 calories for a full rack, they are definitely a meal meant for a serious appetite!

### How to Order Your Steak at Applebee's

To ensure you get the perfect meal, it is important to communicate exactly how you want your steak cooked. Here is a quick guide to steak temperatures at Applebee's:

- **Rare:** Cool red center. The meat is very soft and extremely juicy. (Best for cuts like Filet or lean Sirloin).
- **Medium Rare:** Warm red center. This is the temperature recommended by most chefs, as it perfectly balances tenderness and a warm interior.
- **Medium:** Warm pink center. The texture is firmer, but the steak retains plenty of moisture.
- **Medium Well:** Slight hint of pink in the center. The steak will be quite firm and less juicy.
- **Well Done:** No pink, cooked all the way through. The meat is very firm. (Note: Well-done steaks, especially leaner cuts like sirloin, can become tough).

### Perfect Pairings

The rich, savory flavors of the Steaks & Ribs menu call for beverages that can hold their own. 

- **For the Ribeye or Ribs:** A full-bodied red wine, such as a Cabernet Sauvignon, is the classic choice. The tannins in the wine help cleanse the palate of the rich fats in the meat.
- **For the Bourbon Street Steak:** Lean into the flavor profile and order an Old Fashioned or a Whiskey Smash. The bourbon in the cocktail will complement the bourbon glaze on the steak perfectly.
- **For the Top Sirloin:** A crisp, refreshing draft beer, like a Blue Moon or a local IPA, offers a great contrast to the savory char of the sirloin without being overly heavy.
