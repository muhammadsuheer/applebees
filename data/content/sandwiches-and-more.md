## Between the Bread: Applebee's Sandwiches & Wraps

When the craving hits for something handheld, hearty, and immensely satisfying, a standard burger isn't always the answer. Sometimes, you need the distinct crunch of toasted sourdough, the soft embrace of a warm flour tortilla, or the golden, crispy bite of a classic chicken sandwich. The **Sandwiches & More** category at Applebee's is dedicated to the art of the perfect sandwich, offering a lineup of comfort food classics and flavorful wraps that are perfect for lunch or a lighter dinner.

Applebee's treats their sandwiches with the same culinary respect as their steaks and entrées. They utilize high-quality proteins, freshly baked breads, and signature sauces to ensure that every bite is balanced and delicious. In this guide, we will explore the towering indulgence of the Clubhouse Grille, the zesty, handheld convenience of the Chicken Fajita Rollup, and the timeless perfection of the Classic Chicken Sandwich.

### The Tower of Comfort: Clubhouse Grille

![A high-end food photography shot of a massive Clubhouse Grille sandwich sliced in half with BBQ ranch dripping down.](/apple-bees-menu/sandwiches-lifestyle.jpg)

![Applebee's Clubhouse Grille Sandwich](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-clubhouse-grille-sandwich.webp)

If you are looking for a sandwich that eats like a massive steakhouse dinner, the **Clubhouse Grille** is the undisputed champion of this menu category. It is a towering, messy, incredibly comforting take on the classic club sandwich, reimagined as a hot, grilled melt.

#### The Meats and Cheeses
A standard club sandwich is usually served cold. Applebee's breaks that tradition by serving the Clubhouse Grille piping hot. It features thick slices of slow-roasted turkey breast and heavily marbled Black Forest ham. 

These premium deli meats are layered between two slices of thick, artisan sourdough bread. Sourdough is chosen specifically for its sturdy structure, allowing it to hold up against the heavy ingredients without falling apart, while its slight tanginess cuts through the rich meats. The sandwich is bound together by two types of melted cheese: sharp Cheddar and smooth Jack.

#### The Finishing Touches
To elevate the sandwich further, it is piled high with crispy applewood-smoked bacon. But the true secret to the Clubhouse Grille is the **Signature BBQ Ranch sauce** and the side of **Honey BBQ dressing**. The BBQ Ranch is spread generously on the toasted sourdough, providing a smoky, creamy, and slightly sweet flavor profile that perfectly complements the salty bacon and ham. 

Served with a mountain of classic French fries, the Clubhouse Grille (at 1080 calories) is a massive, highly satisfying meal that perfectly bridges the gap between a sandwich and a full entrée.

### The Handheld Fiesta: Chicken Fajita Rollup

![Applebee's Chicken Fajita Rollup Wrap](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-chicken-fajita-rollup-wrap.webp)

For diners who love the zesty, bold flavors of Mexican cuisine but want the convenience of a clean, handheld sandwich, the **Chicken Fajita Rollup** is the perfect solution. It is arguably one of the most popular lunch items in Applebee's history.

#### The Fillings
The core of the rollup is a generous portion of smoky, grilled chicken breast that has been sliced thin. The chicken is mixed with a traditional fajita blend of blistered, slightly charred red onions and sweet green peppers. This mixture provides the unmistakable, savory aroma of a sizzling fajita platter.

#### The Rollup and Dip
The chicken and peppers are heavily smothered in a melted blend of Cheddar and Jack cheeses, ensuring the wrap holds together beautifully. The entire mixture is then rolled tightly in a warm, soft flour tortilla.

What makes this item a true fan favorite is the side of **Mexi-ranch dipping sauce**. As discussed in our Chicken category, this proprietary sauce is a blend of buttermilk ranch and southwestern spices. Dipping the warm, savory rollup into the cool, creamy, and slightly spicy Mexi-ranch is a deeply addictive culinary experience. At 1030 calories, it is a flavorful and satisfying option that won't leave you feeling overly sluggish.

### The Timeless Standard: Classic Chicken Sandwich

![Applebee's Classic Chicken Sandwich](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-classic-chicken-sandwich.webp)

In the ongoing "Chicken Sandwich Wars" of the fast-food and casual dining industry, Applebee's relies on a tried-and-true formula: high-quality ingredients executed perfectly. The **Classic Chicken Sandwich** is a no-nonsense, incredibly satisfying take on a diner staple.

#### The Chicken
Diners have the option to order the chicken breast either grilled or crispy. The crispy option is heavily battered and deep-fried to a golden brown, providing a massive, satisfying crunch that rivals the best fast-food chicken sandwiches. The grilled option is leaner, featuring a smoky char and a juicy interior.

#### The Build
The chicken is served on a soft, toasted Brioche bunthe same premium bun used for their Handcrafted Burgers. It is topped simply with fresh, crisp iceberg lettuce, thick slices of ripe tomato, red onions, and pickles. A generous slathering of classic mayonnaise provides the necessary moisture and richness to tie the sandwich together. 

At 990 calories (for the crispy version), it is a reliable, comforting, and flawlessly executed sandwich that hits the spot every single time.

### Customization and Pairings

The Sandwiches & More menu is highly flexible. Because these items are served with French fries by default, they offer a great opportunity to customize your sides.

- **Upgrade Your Sides:** For a small upcharge, you can swap the classic fries for Applebee's legendary Onion Rings (served with a side of BBQ sauce) or a house side salad.
- **Soup and Sandwich:** If you are visiting for lunch, ask about the "Lunch Combos." You can often pair a half-portion of the Clubhouse Grille or the Chicken Fajita Rollup with a bowl of French Onion soup or Tomato Basil soup for the ultimate comfort meal.
- **Wrap It Up:** If you love the ingredients of the Classic Chicken Sandwich but want to skip the heavy brioche bun, you can ask your server to have it prepared as a lettuce wrap or folded into a flour tortilla.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Can I get the Clubhouse Grille without the BBQ sauce?**
Yes. The BBQ Ranch is a defining flavor of the sandwich, but if you prefer a more traditional club sandwich experience, you can ask to substitute classic mayonnaise or honey mustard instead.

**Are the onions and peppers in the Fajita Rollup spicy?**
No, they are sweet bell peppers and red onions. They provide a savory, slightly sweet flavor and a soft texture, but they do not contain any heat. 

**Is the sourdough bread toasted?**
Yes, the thick-cut sourdough on the Clubhouse Grille is grilled/toasted to provide structural integrity and a buttery crunch.
