## Innovation on the Menu: Applebee's New Items

While Applebee's will always be anchored by its legendary staplesthe Fiesta Lime Chicken, the Spinach & Artichoke Dip, and the Ribletsthe culinary team is constantly experimenting with new flavor profiles and trends. The **New Items** category is where this innovation is showcased. 

Diners who frequent the restaurant know that checking this section is a must, as it often features limited-time offers (LTOs) and bold new concepts that push the boundaries of traditional neighborhood bar-and-grill fare. In this guide, we are looking at two of the newest heavy hitters that have recently taken the menu by storm.

---

### The Ultimate Indulgence: Whole Lotta Bacon Burger

![Applebee's Whole Lotta Bacon Burger](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-whole-lotta-bacon-burger-menu-item.webp)

Bacon has always been a staple on the Applebee's menu, but the **Whole Lotta Bacon Burger** takes that obsession to an entirely new, unapologetic level. 

#### The Construction
The foundation is Applebee's standard, high-quality, 100% beef patty, cooked to your preference and served on a toasted Brioche bun. However, instead of just laying a couple of strips of bacon on top of the cheese, this burger integrates bacon into every single aspect of the bite.

The patty is topped with melted cheese and a massive portion of thick-cut, crispy applewood-smoked bacon. To amplify the savory, smoky flavor even further, the burger often features a proprietary bacon-infused sauce or bacon jam spread on the bun, ensuring that the primary flavor profile hits you immediately. 

#### The Details
- **Price:** $15.99
- **Calories:** 1150 Cals
- **The Verdict:** This is not a subtle burger. It is a 1150-calorie powerhouse designed specifically for diners who believe there is no such thing as "too much bacon." Served with classic French fries, it is a massive, premium entrée that challenges even the biggest appetites.

### The Heat Wave: Sweet & Spicy Crispy Chicken Sandwich

![Applebee's Sweet & Spicy Crispy Chicken Sandwich](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-sweet-spicy-crispy-chicken-sandwich.webp)

The "Chicken Sandwich Wars" of the last few years forced every major restaurant chain to elevate their poultry game. Applebee's responded brilliantly with the **Sweet & Spicy Crispy Chicken Sandwich**.

#### The Flavor Profile
The star of the show is a massive, thick-cut chicken breast that is heavily battered and deep-fried to achieve a satisfying, audible crunch. Instead of a standard mayonnaise or plain buffalo sauce, the chicken is smothered in a sticky, complex glaze that perfectly balances intense chili heat with a sweet, honey-like finish. 

The heat builds pleasantly as you eat, while the sweetness prevents it from becoming overwhelming. It is typically served on a toasted bun with crisp pickles to add a necessary vinegary snap that cuts through the heavy glaze.

#### The Details
- **Price:** $13.99
- **Calories:** 980 Cals
- **The Verdict:** If you are tired of the standard, plain crispy chicken sandwiches available at fast-food drive-thrus, this is the massive upgrade you have been looking for. The complex glaze elevates the sandwich into a premium dining experience.

### Why Check the "New Items" Section?

The items featured here represent the cutting edge of Applebee's culinary development. Often, these items start as limited-time seasonal promotions. If they perform exceptionally well and generate enough fan feedback, they are occasionally moved to the permanent menu. By ordering from the New Items section, you are essentially participating in a nationwide taste-test, experiencing the boldest and newest flavors the brand has to offer before anyone else.
