## The Ultimate Guide to the Applebee's Kid's Menu: Pricing, Options, and Value

When parents search for **"family friendly restaurants near me,"** they are usually looking for three specific things: an atmosphere where a noisy toddler won't draw glares from other tables, food that their notoriously picky eaters will actually consume, and a bill that doesn't require a second mortgage. 

Applebee's has built its entire "neighborhood grill" reputation on being a welcoming environment for families. But the true hero for parents is the **Applebee's Kid's Menu**. Designed for children aged 12 and under, this menu focuses on familiar, comforting staples served in appropriate portions at highly competitive prices. In this guide, we will break down exactly what is on the menu, the exact costs involved, and answer the internet's most burning questions about family dining at Applebee's.

---

### What is on the Applebee's Kid's Menu?

The most common search query for parents planning a night out is simply wanting to know if a restaurant has something their child will eat. Applebee's avoids overly complex dishes on their children's menu, sticking instead to the undisputed champions of childhood dining. 

Every Kid's Meal at Applebee's includes the main entrée, a choice of one side dish (like French fries, applesauce, or steamed broccoli), and a kid-sized beverage (milk, juice, or a fountain drink). 

Here is the exact breakdown of the most popular items:

#### 1. Kid's Chicken Tenders
![Applebee's Kids Chicken Tenders Meal](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-kids-chicken-tenders-meal.webp)
- **Price:** $6.99
- **Calories:** 450 Cals (before sides)
- **The Details:** This is the gold standard for picky eaters. You receive a generous portion of Applebee's famous golden-fried, crispy chicken tenders. They are mild in flavor, heavily breaded for maximum crunch, and served with a side of classic ranch or honey mustard for dipping. 

#### 2. Kid's Macaroni & Cheese
![Applebee's Kids Macaroni and Cheese](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-kids-macaroni-and-cheese.webp)
- **Price:** $6.49
- **Calories:** 520 Cals (before sides)
- **The Details:** Forget the powdered cheese from a box. This is a rich, creamy, and deeply comforting bowl of macaroni smothered in a thick cheese sauce. It is soft, easy for younger toddlers to chew, and universally beloved by kids of all ages. At $6.49, it is also the most affordable entrée on the menu.

#### 3. Kid's Cheeseburger
![Applebee's Kids Cheeseburger Meal](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-kids-cheeseburger-meal.webp)
- **Price:** $7.49
- **Calories:** 680 Cals (before sides)
- **The Details:** For the older kids with a bigger appetite, the Kid's Cheeseburger is essentially a slightly scaled-down version of the adult classic. It features a juicy, well-done 100% beef patty topped with a slice of melted American cheese on a toasted bun. It is a substantial, filling meal that mirrors what mom and dad are eating.

### Applebee's Kids Menu Prices: Is it a Good Deal?

When analyzing **"cheap family dining"** options, the math at Applebee's is incredibly favorable. 

Because every kid's meal includes the entrée, a side dish, and a drink, the all-in cost for feeding a child ranges between **$6.49 and $7.49** (excluding tax and tip). 

To put that in perspective, a standard kid's meal at a fast-food drive-thru currently averages between $5.00 and $6.50. For roughly $1.00 more, your child is receiving a freshly prepared meal, served on real dishware, in a full-service, temperature-controlled dining room where someone else is doing the dishes. The value proposition for parents is outstanding.

### Healthy Sides and Customizations

While chicken tenders and fries are staples, many parents search for **"healthy kids meals"** when eating out. Applebee's is highly accommodating to these requests. 

If you want to balance out the calories of a cheeseburger or mac and cheese, you do not have to order French fries. Applebee's offers several healthier side alternatives at no extra charge:
- **Steamed Broccoli:** A great way to sneak in some green vegetables.
- **Applesauce:** A sweet, natural side that toddlers love.
- **Side Salad:** For older children who enjoy greens.

Additionally, you can swap the sugary fountain sodas for 100% apple juice, orange juice, or 1% milk, ensuring the meal fits your family's nutritional guidelines.

### The Big Question: Do Kids Eat Free at Applebee's?

This is one of the highest-volume search queries regarding the brand: **"Kids eat free Applebee's."** 

Here is the 100% accurate, assumption-free answer: **It depends on your location and the day of the week.**

Applebee's restaurants are primarily franchise-owned. This means that individual owners have the power to set their own local promotions. Many Applebee's locations *do* offer a "Kids Eat Free" (or Kids Eat for $1.99) promotion, typically on a traditionally slow night like a Tuesday or Wednesday. 

The standard rule for these promotions is usually that you receive one free Kid's Meal with the purchase of one adult entrée. However, because this is not a nationwide corporate mandate, **you must call your local Applebee's or check their specific location page online to confirm if and when they offer this deal.**
