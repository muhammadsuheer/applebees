## Inside Applebee's Appetizers

Appetizers represent the core of Applebee's social dining identity. Designed primarily as shareable starters, the category balances traditional bar snacks with lighter Asian-fusion bites. 

What a first-time orderer needs to know: **almost all single-item appetizers qualify for Applebee's famous half price appetizer promotion** during afternoon and late-night happy hour windows. However, multi-item combination platters like The Classic Combo are strictly excluded from half-price discounts. If you are dining in a pair, ordering two individual appetizers during happy hour is consistently cheaper than ordering a single combo platter.

### What the Nutritional Data Shows

The Chicken Quesadilla carries the most protein at 49g — more than Boneless Wings, which most people assume is the protein pick. Mozzarella Sticks have the best ratio at 41g for 860 calories. The biggest variable is sauce: Classic Hot Buffalo wings carry 4,720mg of sodium against 2,710mg for Garlic Parmesan, despite the Garlic Parmesan version having more calories. Sauce selection determines your meal's sodium level far more than the base chicken.

## Detailed Appetizer Item Breakdowns

### Boneless Wings

![Boneless wings tossed in honey BBQ sauce with dipping sauce](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-boneless-wings-honey-bbq.webp)

Boneless Wings are the single most popular appetizer at Applebee's. Made from tender, all-white-meat chicken breast pieces, they are lightly breaded, fried to a golden crisp, and tossed in your choice of signature sauce: Classic Hot Buffalo, Honey BBQ, Sweet Asian Chile, Garlic Parmesan, or Extra Hot Buffalo. 

A standard order runs **$12.99** and ranges from **860 to 1,100 calories** depending entirely on sauce choice. Each order is served with crisp celery sticks and your choice of house ranch or bleu cheese dressing. They qualify for the half price appetizer promotion during happy hour. For takeout and delivery, always request sauce on the side to prevent trapped steam from softening the crispy coating during transit.

### Mozzarella Sticks

![Golden fried mozzarella sticks with marinara dipping sauce](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-mozzarella-sticks-with-marinara.webp)

Applebee's Mozzarella Sticks are a classic staple consisting of eight thick-cut mozzarella cheese sticks coated in Italian-seasoned breadcrumbs and fried golden brown. Priced at **$9.99** with **860 calories**, they deliver a remarkable **41 grams of protein**, giving them the highest protein-per-calorie efficiency in the starter lineup.

They come paired with a warm, tangy marinara sauce that cuts cleanly through the richness of the melted cheese. Mozzarella sticks are a featured item during half-price happy hour windows. When reheating leftovers at home, an air fryer at 360°F for three minutes restores full crunch without cheese blowout.

### Spinach & Artichoke Dip

![Creamy warm spinach and artichoke dip with crisp tortilla chips](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-spinach-artichoke-dip-appetizer.webp)

The Spinach & Artichoke Dip is Applebee's signature creamy shareable starter. It features a rich, bubbling blend of tender spinach, chopped artichoke hearts, and melted parmesan and mozzarella cheeses. At **$10.49** and **990 calories**, it is accompanied by a generous basket of warm, freshly fried white corn tortilla chips and house salsa.

This dip easily serves three to four guests. It holds heat exceptionally well, making it one of the few appetizers that travels safely for takeout without losing textural integrity. It is eligible for half-price happy hour discounts at participating locations.

### Chicken Quesadilla

![Warm grilled chicken quesadilla with melted cheese and bacon](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-chicken-quesadilla-appetizer.webp)

The Chicken Quesadilla is a hearty Mexican-inspired starter featuring a warm, grilled flour tortilla packed with chipotle-lime grilled chicken, melted Monterey Jack and cheddar cheeses, crispy Applewood-smoked bacon, and fresh pico de gallo. Priced at **$11.99** and **1,170 calories**, it delivers **49 grams of protein** — the highest of any standalone appetizer.

It is served with sides of sour cream and zesty salsa. Because of its density and high protein content, many diners order it as a standalone light entrée rather than a shared starter. It is included in the half-price happy hour promotion.

### Chicken Wonton Tacos

![Crispy chicken wonton tacos with tangy slaw and cilantro](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-chicken-wonton-tacos-appetizer.webp)

Chicken Wonton Tacos represent Applebee's most innovative Asian-fusion starter. Four crispy, folded wonton shells are filled with tangy grilled chicken tossed in sweet Asian chile sauce, topped with crunchy Asian slaw and fresh cilantro. 

At **$11.49** and just **590 calories**, this dish is the lightest appetizer on the entire menu. The crispy wonton shells provide an audible crunch while the slaw balances the sweet heat of the chile glaze. Due to the delicate nature of fried wonton wrappers, this item is best enjoyed dine-in immediately after preparation.

### Brew Pub Pretzels & Beer Cheese Dip

![Warm soft brew pub pretzel sticks with white cheddar beer cheese dip](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-brew-pub-pretzels-beer-cheese-dip.webp)

Brew Pub Pretzels feature four warm, pillowy soft pretzel sticks lightly brushed with butter and sprinkled with coarse pretzel salt. Priced at **$10.99** and **1,160 calories**, they are served alongside a warm beer cheese dip crafted with real Blue Moon® Belgian White ale, accompanied by sweet honey Dijon mustard.

The malty wheat ale notes in the cheese dip complement the fluffy pretzel dough. This dish is vegetarian-friendly and included in half-price appetizer promotions.

### The Classic Combo

![Large appetizer platter with boneless wings, mozzarella sticks, quesadillas, and spinach dip](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-classic-combo-appetizer-trio-platter.webp)

The Classic Combo is Applebee's heavyweight sharing platter designed for groups of four or more. Priced at **$18.99** and **2,200 calories**, it brings four top starters onto one massive tray: Boneless Wings tossed in your favorite sauce, Mozzarella Sticks, a half-order of Spinach & Artichoke Dip with chips, and Chicken Quesadilla slices.

It comes with marinara, ranch, salsa, and sour cream dipping sauces. **Important pricing note:** The Classic Combo is an all-in-one sampler and is excluded from half-price promotions.

## Dietary & Allergen Guidance

- **Vegetarian Options:** Mozzarella Sticks, Brew Pub Pretzels with Beer Cheese, and Spinach & Artichoke Dip contain no meat products.
- **Lightest Pick:** Chicken Wonton Tacos at 590 calories offer the leanest profile.
- **Cross-Contact Notice:** All fried appetizers share common fryer oil with seafood, wheat, and poultry. Diners with severe celiac or peanut/tree nut allergies should consult our complete [nutrition and allergen guide](/nutrition) before ordering.

## Half-Price Happy Hour Deals

Applebee's half-price appetizer promotion runs twice daily at participating locations:
1. **Afternoon Window:** Typically 3:00 PM – 6:00 PM Monday through Friday.
2. **Late-Night Window:** Typically 9:00 PM to close on weeknights, and 10:00 PM to close on weekends.

Core single-item appetizers (Boneless Wings, Mozzarella Sticks, Spinach Dip, Wonton Tacos, Quesadilla, and Pretzels) are discounted by 50% for dine-in guests. Full promotional rules and state drink exemptions are covered on our [happy hour guide](/happy-hour) and [specials and deals page](/specials-and-deals).

## Takeout & Delivery Tips

Fried appetizers lose crispness when sealed in steam-trapping containers during transit. When ordering [takeout](/takeout) or [delivery](/delivery):
- Request dipping sauces and wing sauces on the side.
- Crack the container lid slightly during your drive home to vent trapped steam.
- Reheat wings or mozzarella sticks in an air fryer at 360°F for 2–3 minutes for immediate restoration of restaurant crunch.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What is the most popular appetizer at Applebee's?
Boneless Wings are the most frequently ordered appetizer at Applebee's nationwide. Guests can customize them with five signature sauces: Classic Hot Buffalo, Honey BBQ, Sweet Asian Chile, Garlic Parmesan, or Extra Hot Buffalo.

### Are Applebee's appetizers half price during happy hour?
Yes. Most core standalone appetizers — including Boneless Wings, Mozzarella Sticks, Spinach & Artichoke Dip, Chicken Quesadillas, and Brew Pub Pretzels — are 50% off during designated afternoon and late-night happy hour windows. Combo samplers are excluded.

### What time do half price appetizers start at Applebee's?
Half-price appetizers generally start between 3:00 PM and 6:00 PM for the afternoon window, and at 9:00 PM or 10:00 PM until closing for the late-night window. Exact hours vary by franchise.

### How many calories are in Applebee's Boneless Wings?
An order of Applebee's Boneless Wings contains between 860 and 1,100 calories depending on the sauce. Classic Hot Buffalo contains 1,080 calories, while Honey BBQ contains approximately 1,020 calories without dipping dressing.

### Is The Classic Combo included in half price appetizers?
No. The Classic Combo is a multi-item sampler platter and is excluded from the 50% off happy hour discount. Ordering individual qualifying appetizers during happy hour provides better value.

### What are the vegetarian appetizer options at Applebee's?
Vegetarian-friendly appetizers include Mozzarella Sticks with marinara, Brew Pub Pretzels with beer cheese dip, and Spinach & Artichoke Dip with tortilla chips. Note that fried items share oil with meat products.

### Which Applebee's appetizer has the most protein?
The Chicken Quesadilla delivers the highest protein on the appetizer menu with 49 grams, followed by Mozzarella Sticks with 41 grams and Boneless Wings with 40 grams.
