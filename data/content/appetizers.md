### The All-Time Classics

There are certain items on the Applebee's appetizer menu that have achieved iconic status. These are the dishes that customers return for time and time again.

#### Spinach & Artichoke Dip

![Applebee's Spinach and Artichoke Dip](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-spinach-and-artichoke-dip-appetizer.webp)

Perhaps the most famous of all Applebee's appetizers is the Spinach & Artichoke Dip. This dish is a masterclass in comfort food. It features a rich, creamy blend of melted cheeses, tender spinach, and artichoke hearts, baked until bubbling hot. Served in a shallow dish, it comes with a generous mountain of freshly fried white corn tortilla chips. 

The magic of this dip lies in its texture and balance. The creaminess of the cheese is perfectly offset by the slightly earthy flavor of the spinach and the tang of the artichokes. It is rich, indulgent, and incredibly satisfying. At 960 calories, it is definitely a dish meant for sharing, making it the perfect centerpiece for a group of friends. 

#### Mozzarella Sticks

![Applebee's Mozzarella Sticks](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-mozzarella-sticks-appetizer.webp)

No appetizer menu is complete without Mozzarella Sticks, and Applebee's does them exceptionally well. These are perfectly breaded and fried to a beautiful golden brown, ensuring a satisfying crunch with every bite. Break one open, and you are greeted with the glorious, stretchy pull of warm mozzarella cheese.

Served with a side of zesty marinara sauce, these mozzarella sticks balance the rich, mild flavor of the cheese with the tangy, herbaceous notes of the tomato sauce. They are a staple of the Late Night menu and a hit with both kids and adults. With 910 calories per serving, they are a hearty starter that pairs brilliantly with a crisp soda or a light beer.

#### Boneless Wings

![Applebee's Boneless Wings](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-boneless-wings-appetizer.webp)

For those who prefer a little meatier start to their meal, the Boneless Wings are an absolute must-try. Applebee's takes tender chunks of white meat chicken, breads them until crispy, and fries them to perfection. You can choose from a variety of flavors, but the classics remain the most popular: Classic Hot Buffalo, Honey BBQ, and Sweet Asian Chile. 

The Buffalo sauce offers a traditional, vinegary kick that is cooled down perfectly by a side of ranch or blue cheese dressing. The Honey BBQ provides a sweet, smoky profile that is universally loved. But for many, the Sweet Asian Chile is the standouta sticky, sweet, and slightly spicy glaze that coats the crispy chicken beautifully.

### Innovative and Bold Flavors

While the classics hold a special place in our hearts, Applebee's is constantly innovating its menu to introduce bold, contemporary flavors that appeal to adventurous eaters.

#### Chicken Wonton Tacos

![Applebee's Chicken Wonton Tacos](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-chicken-wonton-tacos-appetizer.webp)

A modern classic, the Chicken Wonton Tacos represent Applebee's ability to create fusion dishes that just work. Instead of traditional tortilla shells, this dish uses crispy wonton wrappers folded into taco shapes. Inside, you will find tangy, grilled chicken tossed in a sweet Asian chile sauce, topped with a vibrant slaw and a sprinkle of cilantro.

These tacos are a textural delightthe crunch of the wonton shell gives way to the tender, flavorful chicken and the crisp freshness of the slaw. They are lighter than many of the fried appetizers, making them an excellent choice if you are planning to tackle a heavy entrée afterward. At 590 calories, they are one of the more calorie-conscious options on the starter menu.

#### Brew Pub Pretzels & Beer Cheese Dip

![Applebee's Brew Pub Pretzels](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-brew-pub-pretzels-appetizer.webp)

Tapping into the popularity of gastropub fare, Applebee's introduced the Brew Pub Pretzels & Beer Cheese Dip. This dish features warm, soft, doughy pretzel sticks, lightly salted and baked to a perfect golden brown. 

The beer cheese dip is made with real Blue Moon® craft beer, giving it a subtle, malty undertone that elevates the sharp cheddar cheese. The dish is also served with a side of honey mustard for a sweet and tangy contrast. The combination of warm, pillowy pretzels and rich, savory cheese is undeniably comforting.

#### Chicken Quesadilla

![Applebee's Chicken Quesadilla](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-chicken-quesadilla-appetizer.webp)

The Chicken Quesadilla is a masterclass in casual Mexican-inspired dining. A large flour tortilla is stuffed with tender grilled chicken, a blend of melted Monterey Jack and Cheddar cheeses, crispy applewood-smoked bacon, and fresh pico de gallo. It is then grilled until the tortilla is crispy and golden.

The addition of bacon to the traditional chicken quesadilla is a brilliant Applebee's twist, adding a smoky, salty crunch that cuts through the richness of the cheese. Served with sides of sour cream and house-made salsa, this 1330-calorie dish is a hefty, flavorful powerhouse that is incredibly satisfying to share.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**What time are Applebee's Half-Price Appetizers?**
Applebee's famous Late Night Half-Price Appetizers promotion typically begins at 9:00 PM or 10:00 PM, depending on the specific location's closing time. During this time, select appetizers are 50% off!

**Are Applebee's Boneless Wings breaded?**
Yes, the boneless wings are heavily breaded and deep-fried to create a crispy exterior that holds onto the sauce perfectly.

**Can I get the Chicken Wonton Tacos without cilantro?**
Yes! All Applebee's items are made to order, so you can absolutely request your Chicken Wonton Tacos without cilantro or slaw.

**What is the lowest calorie appetizer?**
The Chicken Wonton Tacos are currently the lowest calorie option on the standard appetizer menu, coming in at 590 calories for a plate.
