When you are seeking true culinary comfort, there is nothing quite like a massive bowl of hot, creamy, cheese-smothered pasta. It is the ultimate feel-good food. At Applebee's, the **Pasta** category is a celebration of rich sauces, generous portions, and unapologetic indulgence. This isn't your light, delicate, minimalist Italian fare; these are hearty, American-Italian fusion dishes designed to fill you up and leave you completely satisfied.

From classic alfredo sauces to innovative, sweet-and-savory mac and cheese creations, the pasta menu at Applebee's offers something for everyone. In this comprehensive guide, we will dive deep into the legendary Four Cheese Mac & Cheese, explore the comforting appeal of their penne and alfredo dishes, and provide tips on how to order and customize your perfect bowl of pasta.

### The Masterpiece: Four Cheese Mac & Cheese with Honey Pepper Chicken

![A highly detailed food photography shot of a large bowl of creamy four cheese mac and cheese, topped with crispy chicken tenders glazed in honey pepper sauce.](/apple-bees-menu/pasta-lifestyle.jpg)

![Applebee's Four Cheese Mac & Cheese with Honey Pepper Chicken](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-four-cheese-mac-and-cheese-with-honey-pepper-chicken.webp)

If there is one dish that perfectly encapsulates Applebee's willingness to create over-the-top, incredibly satisfying fusion dishes, it is the **Four Cheese Mac & Cheese with Honey Pepper Chicken**. This dish started as an experiment combining two highly successful menu items, and it quickly became one of the most beloved entrées in the restaurant's history.

#### The Mac & Cheese Base
The foundation of this dish is a massive bowl of tender penne pasta tossed in a luxurious four-cheese cream sauce. The cheese blend typically includes Parmesan, Asiago, White Cheddar, and Romano, creating a sauce that is sharp, nutty, and incredibly thick. Unlike a standard boxed mac and cheese, this sauce clings to every single noodle, ensuring that every bite is rich and decadent.

#### The Honey Pepper Chicken
What elevates this dish from simply "good" to "legendary" is what sits on top of the pasta. Applebee's takes their famous crispy, golden-fried chicken tenders and glazes them in their signature Honey Pepper sauce. As we discussed in the Chicken category, this sauce is a complex blend of sweet honey, savory soy, and sharp black pepper. 

When you place the sticky, sweet-and-spicy chicken on top of the rich, salty, four-cheese pasta, culinary magic happens. The sweetness of the honey cuts perfectly through the heavy richness of the cheese sauce. 

#### The Bacon Finish
As if the dish needed any more flavor, it is finished with a generous sprinkling of crispy applewood-smoked bacon. The bacon provides a necessary textural crunch and a hit of salty smokiness that brings the entire dish together. At 1540 calories, it is one of the heaviest items on the menu, but it is worth every single bite.

### The Classic: Classic Broccoli Chicken Alfredo

![Applebee's Classic Broccoli Chicken Alfredo](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-broccoli-chicken-alfredo-pasta.webp)

For those who prefer a more traditional Italian-American dining experience, the **Classic Broccoli Chicken Alfredo** is a flawless execution of a beloved standard. It is simple, comforting, and intensely satisfying.

#### The Alfredo Sauce
The secret to a great Alfredo lies in its simplicity. Applebee's sauce is a rich emulsion of heavy cream, butter, and grated Parmesan cheese. It is seasoned simply with salt, pepper, and a hint of garlic. The result is a velvety smooth sauce that coats the fettuccine noodles perfectly.

#### The Additions
To provide balance and texture, the pasta is tossed with tender, steamed broccoli florets. The slight bitterness and crunch of the broccoli help cut through the heavy fat of the cream sauce. Finally, a perfectly grilled, juicy chicken breast is sliced and placed on top. The grilled chicken adds a necessary smoky, savory element to the dish, making it a complete, well-rounded meal. 

At 1410 calories, it is a hearty dinner that pairs beautifully with a glass of crisp white wine, such as a Pinot Grigio or a Chardonnay.

### The Cheesy Favorite: Three-Cheese Chicken Penne

![Applebee's Three-Cheese Chicken Penne](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-three-cheese-chicken-penne-pasta.webp)

If you cannot decide between a rich cream sauce and a zesty tomato sauce, the **Three-Cheese Chicken Penne** offers the best of both worlds. This dish is a masterclass in comforting, cheesy indulgence.

#### The Sauce and Cheese
The base of this dish is tender penne pasta tossed in a rich, Asiago-Parmesan cream sauce, similar to the Alfredo. However, what sets it apart is the addition of diced tomatoes and a heavy blanket of white cheddar cheese that is melted over the top. The acidity of the tomatoes brightens up the heavy cream, preventing the dish from becoming overly rich.

#### The Protein and Bread
The pasta is mixed with sliced, grilled chicken breast, providing a hearty dose of protein. But perhaps the most exciting part of ordering any pasta dish at Applebee's is the accompanying **Signature Breadstick**. 

Applebee's breadsticks are legendary in their own right. They are brushed with garlic butter and toasted until golden brown on the outside, while remaining incredibly soft and pillowy on the inside. They are the perfect vehicle for soaking up any leftover cheese sauce at the bottom of your bowl.

### Customizing Your Pasta Experience

Applebee's prides itself on being a highly accommodating kitchen, and their pasta dishes offer several avenues for customization.

- **Protein Swaps:** If you are not in the mood for chicken, you can often substitute grilled or blackened shrimp on the Alfredo or the Three-Cheese Penne for an additional charge. 
- **Going Meatless:** You can easily request any of these dishes without the chicken or bacon for a fantastic, cheese-forward vegetarian meal. 
- **Extra Sauce:** If you love a really "wet" pasta dish, don't hesitate to ask your server for an extra side of Alfredo or Four-Cheese sauce.

### Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

**Can I substitute a different type of pasta noodle?**
Applebee's kitchens are typically stocked with Penne and Fettuccine. If a dish usually comes with Penne (like the Mac & Cheese), you can ask to substitute Fettuccine, and vice versa. 

**Are the pasta dishes available in half-portions?**
Applebee's does not typically offer "half-portions" on their main menu. However, if you are looking for a smaller meal, they often run Lunch Combo specials that feature smaller portions of their famous pasta dishes alongside a soup or salad.

**Do the pasta dishes come with a side salad?**
The pasta entrées come standard with a signature garlic breadstick. Side salads (like a house salad or Caesar salad) can be added to your meal for a small upcharge, and they are highly recommended as they provide a fresh, acidic contrast to the heavy pasta.
