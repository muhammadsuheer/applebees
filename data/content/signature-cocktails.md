## $6 Spooky Sips: Applebee's Halloween Drinks

- **Tipsy Zombie, $6:** Bacardí Superior rum with passion fruit, pineapple, cherry and lime, topped with gummy brains.
- **Dracula's Juice, $6:** Bacardí Superior rum, Cuervo Tradicional Blanco, prickly pear and lemon sour.
- **Tropical Spell Mocktail, $4:** pineapple, passion fruit, cherry, lime and dragon fruit, no alcohol.
- **Graveyard Dirty Soda:** Mountain Dew with blue raspberry, a creamy swirl, whipped cream, grenadine and OREO cookie crumbles.

You can add a Patrón Silver "Vibe Drop" to either cocktail. Against a regular $9.99 to $11.99 signature cocktail, a Spooky Sip saves you $4 to $6 a drink, which makes it the best-priced cocktail at Applebee's until mid-November.

## Is the Applebee's Dollarita Back?

No. The $1 Dollarita, a house margarita, came back for July 2026 and ended July 31. Applebee's first ran it in 2017, and it has returned since through its drink-of-the-month promotions, so another run is likely. There's no announced date. For now, the cheapest cocktails are the $6 Spooky Sips and the $6 Bacardí rum cocktail.

## Which Applebee's Drink to Order

**On a budget:** a $6 Spooky Sip until November 15.

**Want a stronger drink?** Promo-priced drinks are built around the price, and they tend to taste sweeter and lighter than the regular cocktails. If strength matters, a cocktail made with a named spirit, like the $10 Don Julio Mucho Margarita, is the safer bet.

**Watching calories:** a Miller Lite draft at 130, or a margarita on the rocks at 310.

**Skip the frozen margarita if you're counting.** Blended, a Mucho Margarita is 650 calories with 75g of sugar, 340 calories more than the same drink on the rocks and within 12g of the sugar in a Triple Chocolate Meltdown. Order it on the rocks and you've saved more calories than most food swaps on the menu.

**For the table:** the Shark Bowl. It's the heaviest cocktail at 420 calories, and nobody orders it for the numbers. It's the blue drink with the gummy shark.

## What "Mucho" Means

Mucho is Applebee's name for its oversized cocktail glass. The drink is bigger and has more mixer and ice in it. For calories, that means a Mucho cocktail lands at 320 to 420, closer to a small dessert than a glass of beer.

## Happy Hour Drinks

Drink discounts at [happy hour](/happy-hour), 3:00 to 6:00 PM and 9:00 PM to close, are set by each restaurant, so there's no national happy hour drink price. Six states ban time-limited drink discounts entirely: Massachusetts, Rhode Island, Vermont, North Carolina, Alaska and Utah. The Spooky Sips are a flat limited-time price, not a happy hour discount.

## Cocktails To Go

Where state law allows it, Applebee's sells cocktails to go at participating restaurants. Expect sealed containers, an ID check at pickup or delivery, and in many states a food order alongside the drinks. Rules change state by state, so check with your [local restaurant](/locations) before ordering.
