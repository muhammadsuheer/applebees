## What's on Applebee's Drink Menu

Applebee's bar menu covers four primary groups. Which specific drinks appear depends on the season and the franchise location:

- **Signature cocktails:** The core range, served in the oversized Mucho glassware the chain is known for. Typically priced between $9.99 and $11.99.
- **Drink of the month:** A rotating featured cocktail at a promotional price. This is where seasonal recipes appear and where the Dollarita periodically returns.
- **Beer and wine:** Draft and bottled beer, plus house wines. These represent the lowest-calorie alcoholic options on the bar menu.
- **Non-alcoholic:** Fountain drinks, fresh-brewed iced tea, flavoured lemonades, and the Dirty Fountain Sodas range. See our [non-alcoholic beverages guide](/menu/non-alcoholic-beverages).

### Reading the table

- **The frozen versions are the outlier:** A frozen margarita runs **650 calories** against **310 calories** for the exact same drink served over ice  the difference is syrup and mix, not alcohol. That is a bigger calorie swing than any food substitution on the entire menu.
- **Draft beer is the lowest-calorie alcoholic option:** A 16 oz light draft at 130 calories is less than half the calories of the lightest signature cocktail.
- **Signature cocktails cluster tightly:** Between 310 and 420 calories, the choice between cocktails is about flavor preference rather than caloric intake. Complete beverage data: [nutrition and calories](/nutrition).

## What Does "Mucho" Mean on Applebee's Menu?

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    Mucho is Applebee's name for its oversized cocktail format  signature drinks served in large goblet-style glassware rather than a standard cocktail glass. The size comes largely from ice and mixers; the spirit measure is standard.
  </p>
</div>

That last point is the one worth understanding: **a larger glass does not mean a stronger drink.** The extra volume is mix, juice, and crushed ice, which is also why the calorie figures land where they do  the sugar scales with the glass while the alcohol volume remains constant.

**What it does mean:** These are drinks built to share a table with food and to last through an entire meal, rather than to be finished quickly. For anyone tracking sugar intake, a Mucho-size cocktail is nutritionally closer to a dessert than to a standard glass of wine.

## What Is the Applebee's Dollarita?

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    The Dollarita is Applebee's house margarita offered at $1.00 as a limited-time promotion. It is not a permanent menu price  it returns periodically, usually for a defined promotional window, rather than running year-round.
  </p>
</div>

Applebee's launched the $1 margarita in late 2017 and it became the promotion the brand is best known for. Since then it has evolved into a broader drink-of-the-month programme, with the original dollar price point returning as a recurring event.

![House margarita served over ice with salted rim and lime](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-dollarita-house-margarita-cocktail.webp)

### What this means practically

- **Do not assume it is running:** The most common frustration is arriving expecting a $1 margarita and finding a $6 featured cocktail instead. Always check with your local restaurant first.
- **It comes back:** When it does return, it is heavily announced and time-limited.
- **Ask about the Drink of the Month:** That programme runs continuously; the Dollarita is one recurring entry in it.
- **Nutritional profile:** At 310 calories, the house margarita over ice is the lightest signature cocktail on the bar menu; the frozen version roughly doubles that figure.

Check current active campaigns on our [specials and deals](/specials-and-deals) guide and view time windows on [happy hour](/happy-hour).

## Applebee's Signature Cocktails

Featured cocktails rotate throughout the year. The drinks below represent recurring, fan-favorite entries:

### Mucho Blue Hawaiian

![Blue tropical rum cocktail in a large goblet glass with pineapple](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-mucho-blue-hawaiian-cocktail.webp)

A tropical rum cocktail made with rum, pineapple juice, and blue curaçao, served in the oversized Mucho glass. Sweet and fruit-forward, it is the most visually recognisable drink in the lineup because of its bright electric blue colour.

At **350 calories**, it sits mid-range for the category. The sweetness works well against spicy dishes  it is a common pairing with the Cajun and chile-glazed entrées on the [chicken menu](/menu/chicken).

- **Price & Nutrition:** $9.99 · 350 calories

### Bacardí Blushing Dragon Mucho

![Pink dragon fruit rum cocktail in an oversized glass](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-bacardi-blushing-dragon-mucho-cocktail.webp)

Bacardí Superior rum combined with exotic dragon fruit, lemon sour, and tart lemonade. It delivers a bright pink hue and a tart, crisp profile rather than purely sweet flavors, making it a great pick for guests who find the Blue Hawaiian too heavy.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $10.00 · 330 calories

### Shark Bowl

![Large blue frozen cocktail served with a gummy shark garnish](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-shark-bowl-signature-drink.webp)

The ultimate presentation drink. A large blue cocktail served with a signature gummy shark floating on top, and in some locations a separate cup of red syrup poured tableside. Ordered as much for the table reaction as for the flavour, making it a fixture at celebrations.

At **420 calories**, it is the heaviest entry in the signature cocktail lineup.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $10.99 · 420 calories

### Top Shelf Long Island Iced Tea

![Long Island iced tea with lemon garnish over ice](/apple-bees-menu/applebees-top-shelf-long-island-iced-tea.webp)

A classic Long Island crafted with premium-brand spirits (vodka, rum, gin, tequila, and triple sec), cola, and fresh lemon. Smooth rather than overly fruity, this is the choice for diners seeking a straightforward, potent cocktail.

- **Price & Nutrition:** $11.99 · 380 calories

### House Margarita

The foundation of the Dollarita promotion and the lightest signature cocktail at **310 calories over ice**. When ordered blended or frozen, it roughly doubles to around **650 calories with 75g of sugar**. 

Choosing between frozen or on the rocks is the single biggest calorie decision on the drink menu.

## Can You Get Applebee's Cocktails To Go?

<div class="answerBlock">
  <p>
    Applebee's offers signature cocktails to go at participating locations where state and local law permits. Drinks are supplied in sealed, tamper-evident containers rather than open cups, and rules vary significantly by state.
  </p>
</div>

Temporary allowances introduced during the pandemic have since expired in some states and been made permanent in others, so availability is genuinely location-dependent rather than a universal policy.

Where permitted, expect:
- **Sealed packaging:** Tamper-evident pouches or sealed cups are a legal safety requirement.
- **A required food order:** Many jurisdictions permit to-go alcohol only when accompanied by a qualifying food purchase.
- **Mandatory ID verification:** Valid 21+ government photo ID is verified at pickup or delivery; alcohol cannot be left unattended.

Verify availability with your [local restaurant](/locations) before placing your order. More on curbside pickup and delivery mechanics: [Carside To Go](/takeout) and [delivery](/delivery).

## Discounted Drinks at Applebee's

Happy hour covers discounted alcoholic beverages alongside half-price appetizers at participating locations, typically during an afternoon window (3:00 PM – 6:00 PM) and again late night.

**Important state exemption:** Six states prohibit time-limited alcohol discounts outright, which means locations in those states cannot discount drinks during happy hour. Instead, those locations usually feature an all-day Drink of the Month promotion, since a constant all-day promotional price is legal where a timed discount is not.

Full state rules, times, and discount structures: see our [happy hour times and menu](/happy-hour) guide.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What drinks does Applebee's have?
Signature cocktails in oversized Mucho glassware, a rotating drink of the month, draft and bottled beer, wine, and non-alcoholic options including fountain drinks, iced tea, lemonades and Dirty Fountain Sodas. Signature cocktails run about $9.99 to $11.99.

### What is a Dollarita at Applebee's?
A house margarita offered at $1.00 as a limited-time promotion rather than a standing menu price. It returns periodically as part of Applebee's drink of the month programme, and is not available year-round.

### Is the Dollarita still available?
It runs as a recurring promotion rather than continuously. The drink of the month programme runs year-round, and the $1 margarita is one entry that returns periodically. Check with your local restaurant for what is currently featured.

### What does Mucho mean at Applebee's?
Mucho is the name for Applebee's oversized cocktail format  signature drinks in large goblet-style glassware. The additional volume comes from ice and mixers rather than from a larger spirit measure.

### How many calories are in Applebee's cocktails?
Signature cocktails run 310 to 420 calories. The house margarita is lightest at 310; the Shark Bowl heaviest at 420. Frozen versions are substantially higher  a frozen margarita reaches around 650 calories.

### How much are Applebee's cocktails?
Signature cocktails cost about $9.99 to $11.99. The Dollarita is $1.00 when the promotion is running. Prices vary by location, since restaurants are independently franchised.

### Can I get Applebee's cocktails to go?
At participating locations where state and local law permits. Drinks are supplied sealed, usually require a food order alongside, and ID verification is required.

### What is the lowest calorie drink at Applebee's?
Among alcoholic options, a 16 oz light draft beer at 130 calories. Fresh-brewed unsweetened iced tea is zero calories. The lightest cocktail is the house margarita over ice at 310.

### Does Applebee's have a drink of the month?
Yes. A rotating featured cocktail runs at a promotional price. What is featured changes regularly, and it is the programme through which the Dollarita periodically returns.
